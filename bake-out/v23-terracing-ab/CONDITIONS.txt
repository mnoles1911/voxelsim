V23 TERRACING CAPTURES -- CONDITIONS
====================================
2026-08-03

WHAT THIS SET IS, AND WHAT IT IS NOT
------------------------------------
Five settled frames of worldgen v23, shot to answer one question: did v23
reintroduce visible terracing?

IT IS NOT AN A/B. It was planned as one -- ten v23 frames against ten matched
v22 frames -- and the v22 arm was DELIBERATELY NOT SHOT. After the first five
v23 frames the owner judged them on the absolute question and approved:

    "No terracing in the v23 screenshots. It looks good."

A comparison exists to answer "is v23 worse than v22". Once the absolute answer
is "there is no terracing here", the comparison has nothing left to decide, so
the remaining v23 poses and the entire v22 arm were cancelled rather than shot
for symmetry. This paragraph is here so that nobody later reads the missing
v22-* files as an unfinished job and "completes" it.

The one v23 frame that is missing and was not cancelled is noted at the bottom.

THE QUESTION THESE FRAMES WERE FOR
----------------------------------
worldgen v23 (claude/fine-micro-cap, 0609be1) fixed a drainage defect by
REDUCING detail amplitude on slopes: it caps the SUM of the two detail pools at
1.0x the carrier gradient, where the previous per-pool caps let the total reach
2.2x. Measured stranded area on the worst clean fine site fell 35.5% -> 3.6%,
and 11.5% -> 0.0% on another.

Less detail amplitude on slopes is the trade v21 made in the OTHER direction, to
break up quantisation banding. So the risk was that v23 bought drainage at the
price of visible terracing, against the owner's standing mandate: "No banding.
We must have 10cm cubic voxels arranged in a natural, nature like way that is
believable to the player." That is what these frames were taken to put in front
of the owner. The verdict above is the owner's, not mine.

WHAT WAS BUILT
--------------
  v23   0609be1  "worldgen v23: cap the SUM of the two detail pools, not each
                  one separately"

Built and shot from an ISOLATED GIT WORKTREE (D:\vox-ab), not from the shared
checkout, and this is not bureaucracy. On the first attempt at this set another
session moved D:\voxelsim from claude/fine-micro-cap onto a docs branch WHILE a
capture was in flight. That reverted voxel-core/shaders/worldgen.ush from 23 to
22 underneath a binary built at 23 -- and nothing in the image, the filename or
the streaming counters would have said so. Those frames were discarded and
re-shot here.

Every capture therefore records the tree state -- HEAD, both worldgen version
constants, and the mtimes of voxelcore.lib and UnrealEditor-VoxelEarth.dll --
read immediately BEFORE and AFTER the shutter. "TREE STABLE: True" in
settle-log.txt means they matched. All five frames below are stable at
head=0609be1, worldgen ush=23 cpp=23.

A BUILD FIX WAS NEEDED TO COMPILE v23 AT ALL. As committed, 0609be1 bumps
vxc::kWorldGenVersion to 23 without moving VoxelGpuVerify.cpp's
kExpectedCpuDigestWorldGenVersion, and its static_assert stops the UE build. It
was re-measured rather than widened: vxc_gpu PASSES bit-exact against the
committed SPIR-V with a freshly built v23 CPU lib, and UE's own RDG path reports
CPU reference digest b2b5d2f1044caa35 -- the value already pinned. So only the
version constant needed to move, which the coordinator has since committed as
bd6b96e. NOTE THE REASON THE DIGEST DID NOT MOVE: both fixture regions are
COARSE tier, and v23's change is gated on `fine`, so the fixtures never execute
the changed branch. The unchanged fold is arithmetically expected, not
reassurance that v23 is inert.

not read on a 1200 m vista, and a vista that looks clean is not evidence about
terracing. The poses are labelled accordingly:

  low120   120 m above the surface, pitch -20 deg. MEDIUM range. This is the

WHY THESE SITES
---------------
  taiga (-7,-2)    The WORST site in the v23 drainage measurement: 39.7% grade,
                   stranded area 35.5% -> 3.6%. The largest amplitude reduction
                   anywhere, therefore the highest terracing risk. It is the
                   site to judge on, and it is the one shot at all three poses.
  tundra (-3,-3)   The other measured site: 59.7% grade, 11.5% -> 0.0%. Also the
                   site the hatching diagnosis used, so this frame is directly
                   comparable to bake-out/hatching-ab/.
  savanna (2,-8)   GENTLE ground, relief 376 m over 6 km. The control: where the
                   engagement ramp barely engages, v23 should change nothing.

All three sit on baked fine tiles. Only 17 of 289 tiles are baked, and the fine
tier is a FATAL residency gate on unbaked ground in unattended runs, so the
camera cannot simply be moved somewhere prettier.

THE SETTLE EVIDENCE
-------------------
settle-log.txt has every counter verbatim. A frame is judgeable only if it
reports

    settle: jobsInFlight=0 pendingJobs=0 unloaded=0

All five frames above do, with 0 undrawn chunks. Any non-zero value means the
world was still streaming at the shutter and the frame must be EXCLUDED, not
argued about; a large "peak loaded" does not redeem it, because a thrashing
world and a large settled world both report big numbers.

THE SIXTH FRAME, WHICH IS NOT A FAILURE
---------------------------------------

NONE". That capture was in flight when job 1 was stopped and I killed the
editor; there is no image and its counters describe a run that never reached its

evidence of anything. Left in the log rather than deleted, because a settle log
