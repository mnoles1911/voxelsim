## Evidence and the current ceiling

`asset-forge/out/ecological-placement/detail-presentation-analysis-1/report.json` describes 339 private models across 113 species: 16 have one LOD, 260 have two, 63 have three. Last-LOD triangles have median 958, p90 29,281 and maximum 129,339. Rhododendron-0007 remains 129,339 triangles from 258,678; elder-0007 remains 117,325. Both large models retain their last LOD at screen size .10. The report estimates 1,144,390,324 packed buffer bytes across all LODs; this excludes CPU copies and is not measured VRAM. Its 80% circle-area reduction estimate is not measured performance.

`VoxelDetailMeshLOD.h:21�55` is an exact coplanar rectangle merger, not a general distant geometry simplifier. It cannot remove voxel stair steps, disconnected leaf faces or surfaces on different planes. Every constituent face must exist; material/class, normal/tangent, affine source UV and wind UV must agree. A merged rectangle gets one mean color within an error tolerance, so approved per-face variation limits merging. The wind interpolation bound must remain below 2.5 mm. `VoxelDetailAssetSubsystem.cpp:879�882` tries only 2x2 and 4x4 maximum patches, with .015/.030 linear color tolerances. The second tier is omitted unless it saves another 20% against the retained predecessor. A missing .025 tier is therefore intentional rejection of weak savings, not failed runtime LOD selection. We have no rejection-reason counts identifying which constraint dominates each shrub.

## Separate experiments

**A: relaxed coplanar merging.** First record rejects by missing face, material, color, UV and wind. Try maximum patch 8 with unchanged color/wind limits; separately measure a modest color-tolerance ladder. Keep source holes and face planes exact. This is inexpensive to prototype and cannot remove thin stems, but its geometric ceiling remains. Do not promise an order-of-magnitude reduction. Increasing the color tolerance erases face variation; increasing patch size alone may be blocked by nonlinear wind. Do not loosen several constraints together or label a color-altered mesh appearance-identical.

**B: a new distant 50 mm presentation mesh.** Recommended substantive pilot for rhododendron, elder and bramble; keep grass, daisy and reed as failure controls. Read the authoritative 25 mm grid without changing VXA/VAC or ecological instances. Build a separate 2x2x2 candidate surface, with deterministic source mapping. Compare representative and occupancy-fraction policies as candidates: neither is accepted a priori. Any-solid pooling fattens crowns and fills holes; majority/representative sampling can erase thin branches and flowers. Retain a 25 mm residual mesh for wood/stems/petals and for blocks whose simplification breaks connected structures or reviewed visible openings. If that hybrid remains too expensive, fail the pilot rather than silently deleting details. Boundary/T-junction handling and overlapping residual faces need explicit tests.

Keep source material and wind class separate. Transfer colors from exposed source faces with matching orientation/material, retaining a deterministic variation pattern and measuring its image statistics; do not choose new random colors or average all volume voxels. Preserve source-space planar mask phase, approved cutout function and spring palette. Derive wind attributes from retained source height/position, then recompute bounds across the complete resulting LOD chain. A coarse mesh cannot claim exact source silhouette or per-face identity.

Treat 100 mm (4x4x4) as a later, separate far-distance experiment, only if 50 mm passes. It has a substantially greater risk of filled gaps and vanished stems; never change the authoritative source pitch to obtain it.

## First acceptance experiment

Keep LOD0 and the existing .10 tier. Trial a 50 mm tier at .025 projected sphere diameter (about 27 pixels at 1080 vertical pixels under the screen-size convention). This is a starting configuration, not a proven transition: actual HISM switching depends on cluster bounds. Measure actual selected LOD at both sides of the transition. A distant-only change may not improve the close foreground shrub cost at all.

Capture matched original/candidate at 16 azimuths, three elevations, several wind times and boundary distances, at 720p/1080p/1440p and narrow/normal/wide FOV. Capture linear albedo, depth/mask, normals and fixed-exposure lit output separately. Require nonempty reference coverage, at least 64 reference pixels for an asset-level comparison, and a same-pose unculled positive control near disappearance; tiny/all-zero frames cannot pass.

Provisional rejection gates: silhouette displacement above one pixel, coverage change above 3%, disappearance of any reference opening or stem wider than two pixels, or obvious temporal popping. Compare interior albedo at common covered pixels and report mean and p95 error plus variation/contrast retention; approve tolerances visually before rollout. A one-pixel/3% threshold is proposed, not historical acceptance evidence. Night and shadow controls must catch inappropriate brightness changes; lighting parity is a separate outstanding issue.

Record per-LOD triangles, vertices, material sections, actual CPU/RHI resource bytes, bake time and HISM GPU costs. Trial resource gate: new distant tier at least 75% fewer triangles than the current last LOD, with added chain bytes at most 15% of current chain bytes. These are pilot targets, not predicted outcomes. Omit weak tiers; do not accumulate several barely different meshes. Preserve all approved inventory and retain full source coverage after the small pilot succeeds.
