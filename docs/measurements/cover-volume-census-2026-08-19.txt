COVER VOLUME CENSUS -- vxc_volumeprobe --detail-cover
=====================================================
Date   2026-08-19
Probe  voxel-core/bench/volumeprobe.cpp, new --detail-cover mode
Seed   20260719, worldgen v28, 438 asset species installed (0 without banks)
Ref    docs/detail-assets-in-the-volume-2026-08-19.md sections 5 and 5.1

WHY IT EXISTS: docs/detail-assets-in-the-volume-2026-08-19.md section 3.1
disputes ray-marching-plan-2026-08-19.md:795-797, which asserts that putting the
230 detail-plant species into a volume and taking R0 to 5 cm are the same work
item. Section 5.1 named the falsifier before the run: a cover volume measuring
~500 MiB or more means the plan is right and the doc is wrong.

WHAT A COVER VOLUME IS HERE: a separate 5 cm lattice holding ONLY detail-lattice
cover -- no terrain. A brick column no instance reaches produces zero bricks,
not an all-air shell. Terrain stays in the 10 cm world volume where it already
is. Composition is AssetField::resolveForCoverCompose / coverMaterialOfResolved
(voxel-core), which is also the byte-equality reference for any GPU mirror.

   payload = descriptors + occupancy + 16 B palette + adaptive cells.

   alpine bank -61440,-61440  real fine+climate      156   10.5 MiB   290.1 MiB

   RING 256 m -- THE RING THAT ACTUALLY SHIPS (VoxelDetailAssetSubsystem.cpp:152,
   raised from 112 on 2026-08-18; 5.2x the area):
   grassland COVER            REAL fine+climate    3,564  131.6 MiB   245.4 MiB
   grassland TERRAIN CONTROL  REAL fine+climate        -  126.7 MiB    67.5 MiB

   The 112 m rows were measured against a default that was already stale when it
   was read out of docs/detail-asset-rendering.md. Measured rather than scaled,
   the shipping ring returned 5.18x the 112 m payload -- exactly the area ratio,
   so cover density is uniform across the ring.

   WHAT THE SHIPPING ROW CHANGES:
     * falsifier margin 3.8x, not 20-48x. The decision stands; the comfort does
       not.
     * "cover is 34% of terrain" DOES NOT SURVIVE. That was alpine ground with
       almost no cover on it (156 inst/ha). On dense grassland at the shipping
       ring cover is 104% of terrain -- 131.6 vs 126.7 MiB -- it roughly DOUBLES
       the resident volume. Honest range: 34% where nothing grows, ~100% where
       things do.
     * requirement C1 gets STRONGER: the flat index is 245.4 MiB against
       131.6 MiB of payload.
     * unchanged: cover + terrain at the shipping ring is ~258 MiB of payload,
       still far under the 1,110 MiB measured for R0-at-5cm and under today's
       2,197 MiB quad pool. Scoping the cover volume is still the cheaper
       project by a wide margin.

   CONTROL, same ground as the alpine arm, same reading:
   terrain at 10 cm, --radius 112       payload 31.3 MiB, flat index 37.4 MiB,
                                        total adaptive 66.9 MiB

   * coarse-only = synthetic elevation + REAL coarse climate, the same
     instrument the biome survey used for 6 of its 10 sites. Named, not hidden:
     biome weight is the dominant placement gate (83.5% of refusals at the
     alpine site), and that gate is real here; elevation band (15.8%) is not.

   VERDICT: the doc STANDS. 10.5-26.5 MiB against a 500 MiB falsifier -- a
   factor of 19-48 clear. On identical ground the cover volume is 34% of what
   the terrain volume already costs there (10.5 vs 31.3 MiB payload).


2. THE SITE ALMOST BURIED THE RESULT, AND THAT IS THE READING RULE
   The first run used the brick census's own site (-61440,-61440). It returned
   10.5 MiB and it is nearly a null result: vxc_assetprobe over the same 112 m
   says only THREE species place there -- alpine-krummholz 600, juniper-scrub
   331, granite-boulder 6 -- with NO grass, NO flowers, NO reeds. It is a
   steep, above-treeline rock face; 83.5% of (site, species) pairs are refused
   by biome weight alone.

   So the headline number from that site is "what cover costs where there is
   almost no cover". The grassland arm is the one that prices cover. A cover
   census at a barren site is not evidence about cover, the same way
   vxc_farwaterprobe #226's meshed zero was not evidence about cost.

   The two sites disagree in BOTH directions and it nearly cancelled:
     - alpine has 24x FEWER instances per hectare (156 vs 3,717)
     - but its instances are 38x BIGGER (8,173 vs 214 solid voxels each --
       juniper-scrub bakes at 5,131-10,900 solid voxels, grass/flowers at
       214-480)
   Reporting either site alone, without the other, would have been a confident
   number about the wrong world.


3. CROSS-CHECKS THAT PASSED
   * Instance density against the independent instrument: this probe reports
     3,717/ha at grassland; docs/biome-placement-survey.md:41 measures 3,908/ha
     for grassland (grass 2043.3 + flower 1850.6 + reed 14.0). 5% apart, from
     two different walks.
   * Per-instance solid voxels against the bank census: 214 at grassland
     against 257 (grass) / 480 (flower) mean solid in the .vxa run tables;
     8,173 at alpine against juniper-scrub's own 5,131 and 10,900.
   * Anchor-ownership dedup: reach dilation returns one instance to every chunk
     it overlaps, so summing per-chunk list sizes over-counts. Counting only
     instances whose ANCHOR lies in the chunk is what makes the density agree
     with the survey; without it the figure read several times high.


4. THE ONE TERM THAT CAN STILL BLOW UP: A DENSE INDEX
   A flat 3D brick index over the cover footprint costs 290.1 MiB at the alpine
   site and 25.4 MiB at grassland -- an 11x swing driven by TERRAIN RELIEF, not
   by cover. Cover is a thin shell following the ground, so a dense index over
   its bounding box is 0.075% occupied on steep ground.

   A COVER VOLUME MUST NOT BE DENSELY INDEXED. This is the same design
   consequence ray-marching-plan section 4 records for 2.5 cm, arrived at from
   the cover side, and it is the only term anywhere near the falsifier. The
   payload columns are the cost of a cover volume; the index column is the cost
   of addressing it the wrong way.


5. A SECONDARY RESULT, FROM THE SAME WALK
   The probe meshes every brick it censuses, so quads and bricks are two
   readings of one field. Cover as a VOLUME costs 26.5 MiB at grassland; the
   same cover as QUADS is 9,074,442 quads = 103.8 MiB at 12 B/quad. The volume
   is 3.9x cheaper than meshing the same cover -- and at the alpine site,
   7,267,620 quads = 83.2 MiB against 10.5 MiB, 7.9x cheaper.


6. REPRODUCTION
   vxc_volumeprobe --detail-cover --cover-ring-m 112 --at -61440 -61440 \
     --assets asset-forge/out/engine --tiles out/volumeprobe-fine \
     --coarse <world>/000000000135276f/s1

   vxc_volumeprobe --detail-cover --cover-ring-m 112 --at -92445 -75855 \
     --assets asset-forge/out/engine --coarse <world>/000000000135276f/s1 \
     --allow-synthetic

   control:
   vxc_volumeprobe --radius 112 --at -61440 -61440 \
     --assets asset-forge/out/engine --tiles out/volumeprobe-fine \
     --coarse <world>/000000000135276f/s1

   A cover census without --assets is refused: every detail species would
   compose as air and the run would report a free volume.


7. STILL OPEN -- AND THE PREDICTION, NOW RESOLVED
   No vegetated FINE-TILE site was censused -- grassland's fine tile (-7_-5) is
   not in the working set, and the survey's staged tiles are gone. The
   grassland number therefore stands on real climate over synthetic elevation.

   PREDICTION, RECORDED BEFORE THE RUN: re-running --detail-cover at
   -92445,-75855 with a real fine tile will land the payload between 15 and
   45 MiB, and not above 100 MiB. Reasoning: at the one site where gate
   attribution was measurable, biome weight refused 83.5% of (site, species)
   pairs and elevation band 15.8%. The grassland run already has real climate,
   which drives the dominant gate; real elevation refines the minority one.

   RESOLVED 2026-08-20: 25.4 MiB. Prediction holds; the coarse-only proxy was
   accurate to 4% (25.4 vs 26.5) and density moved 3,717 -> 3,611/ha, still
   within 8% of the survey's 3,908.

   NO BAKE WAS NEEDED. -7_-5.vxtl already existed in the v28 fine namespace UE
   is pinned to (tile-cache/...-b19d281fd/000000000135276f/s16/, 312 MB,
   2026-08-18), and the grassland site sits inside it: tile edge 15,360 m, so
   (-7,-5) covers X -107,520..-92,160 and Y -76,800..-61,440. The gap was a
   staging oversight, not a missing measurement. Worth remembering the next
   time a result is qualified as "blocked on a bake".

   STILL UNMEASURED: every biome other than grassland and alpine on real fine
   tiles. By this file's own reading rule (section 2) that is a limit on the
   claim, not a caveat to wave at it.
