READ THE ROWS THAT DID *NOT* MOVE FIRST.

  * The ROOF is 6.0 m in both arms, against a 6.0 m clamp. That is the number
    the v27 calibre widening put at risk: a 4.0 m tube cannot sit 9 m down
    under a 6 m clamp by arithmetic alone, so the guarantee moved from a
    two-constant static_assert into a per-control-value cap
    (caves.h caveScaledRadiusMm) plus a linearity argument. This row is the
    measurement that the cap holds on real geometry, and it holds exactly --
    the clamp is still a backstop, not the mechanism.

  * ENTRANCES BARELY MOVED, and that is a real (negative) result rather than a
    rounding artifact. 20 sites open at v26's flat 1-in-4, 19 at the field's
    1-in-8..1-in-3. The SET changed -- four sites the control reports as
    "GATED OPEN BUT SEALED" are closed in the shipping arm and three others
    opened -- but the COUNT did not, because this fixture's relief at the node
    anchors sits near the middle of the ramp, where the field's gate is close
    to 1-in-4 by construction. The entrance-density half of W5 is therefore
    NOT demonstrated by this fixture, and no claim is made for it here. It
    needs a run over real tiles spanning a plain and a range; see OUTSTANDING.

  * PERFORATION moved 5%, i.e. essentially not at all, which is the point: the
    surface is not more of a colander at v27 than at v26 on this ground.

WHAT DID MOVE IS THE TUNNEL AND FRACTURE VOLUME: 2.06x and 3.28x on
high-relief synthetic ground. The two terms behind them are separable in the
table -- axis LENGTH is up 1.20x (that is the density gate, more non-backbone
edges opening) and volume is up 2.06x, so the remaining 1.7x is CALIBRE. Both
are doing work, and the calibre term is the larger of the two.

GLOBAL VOLUME IS WITHIN BUDGET, treated as a gate rather than a note: 2.727%
of the 6..45 m subsurface band is cave air, against the 15% ceiling
cave_volume_fraction_is_cave_like_not_sponge_like enforces -- 5.5x of headroom,
and the ctest reads 2.816% on its own footprint. The band's DEPTH envelope did
not move at all (caves.h kCaveDeepestCarveMm, 41.0 m, still set by the crevice
down-reach rather than by the widened tube).

---------------------------------------------------------------------------
THE NOISE FLOOR, AND ONE INSTRUMENT GAP FOUND WHILE MEASURING
---------------------------------------------------------------------------
Two of the rows above are bounded below by the construct rather than by the
field, and saying so is the point:

  * entrance VOLUME cannot move much in either direction, because the cavity's
    footprint is deliberately NOT field-scaled (see caves.h "G5 MOUTH BUDGET"
    for the measurement that dropped that term), so 0.98x is the expected null,
    not a weak signal.

  * plan components (3 vs 3) and the two cavern rows (2 open sites in both
    arms) are unaffected BY CONSTRUCTION -- W5 couples G1/G3/G5 and leaves G2
    to W6 -- so they are controls on the control, not results.

AND ONE LINE IN THAT OUTPUT IS NOT AN A/B AT ALL, WHICH IS WORTH RECORDING
RATHER THAN QUIETLY DROPPING. The `cave-only flood fill` block reports
byte-identical numbers in both arms (components=4, 74663 samples, largest
66.82%). That is not a null result: `--field-off` is wired through
caveColumnVariantsFor and through the site-gate helper, but the connectivity
section reduces columns through the shipping `Amplifier` directly, so it sees
the shipping arm in BOTH runs. The connectivity effect of the density gate is
therefore UNMEASURED, not measured-as-zero. It is exactly the shape of error
W3's mouth metric and W4's first floor statistic were -- a statistic satisfied
by the construct rather than by the thing under test -- and it is listed under
OUTSTANDING rather than reported.

The structural argument for connectivity is unchanged and does not depend on
that measurement: the density gate only ever moves the 1-in-N rate on
NON-BACKBONE edges, and the backbone is unconditional, so the network stays
connected by construction for every seed even if the density field went to
zero. That is a property of the formulation, not of a threshold.

CPU/GPU
---------------------------------------------------------------------------
vxc_gpu PASSES bit-exact at kWorldGenVersion 27 on an AMD Radeon RX 7800 XT,
59,520 columns / 18,843,648 cells / 14,626 quads over 12 regions, digest
ec45911f93ad4c47 (v26 was 68acd4a2d65f4d0f). The cave-band fixture's exercised
count rose 2027 -> 2439 cave columns of 4096 and the entrance fixture's 9691 ->
11120, i.e. the fixtures do reach the changed code.

VoxelizeMain now binds ClimatePacked (t1 -> Vulkan binding 2) alongside
ElevationMm, because the field taps are per lattice NODE: the CPU memoises them
per lattice cell (65'536 columns) and the shader cannot. Cost measured on the
harness: VoxelizeMain total dispatch 11.32 ms -> 14.16 ms over the same 18.8M
cells (1664 -> 1331 Mcells/sec). That is 16 climate loads plus 64 raw elevation
loads per thread and it is the honest price of the wave.
