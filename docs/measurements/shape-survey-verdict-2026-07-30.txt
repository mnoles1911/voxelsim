DEFECTS NAMED BY THE OWNER, who was judging physical voxel shape only (colour

  g25-third (24.6%)  a few odd blue specks
  g26       (25.9%)  blue specks, and noticeable ridges in the terrain
  g35       (35.0%)  a CLEAR PATTERN OF JUMP RIDGES IN A STRAIGHT LINE
  g43       (43.0%)  vertical ridge jumps every metre or two, as in the others

THE SPACING ESTIMATE IS THE MOST USEFUL PART, and it is why this is written down
rather than filed as "looks a bit regular". An independent estimate of "every
metre or two" lands on a 1.6 m periodicity, and there are exactly two terms in the
client at that wavelength:

  (a) the rill/flute term  -- kRillAcrossMm = 1600, warped 8:1 along the local
      fall line, so its pattern should follow the slope as CHEVRONS; and
  (b) the 1600 mm microrelief octave -- {1600, 500} in both kDetailOctaves and
      kFineDetailOctaves, ordinary value noise on an AXIS-ALIGNED world lattice,
      whose visible structure runs along world x/y as STRAIGHT LINES.

The owner's word for g35 was "straight line", not chevron. That discriminates
between the two, and it points at (b) rather than at (a) -- which is the opposite
of the hypothesis I offered when sending the frames, where I named the rill term as
the suspect on the strength of the chevrons I thought I saw in g41 and g43.

So the direction is: ABLATE BOTH AND RE-CAPTURE THE SAME FRAME, rather than fix
the term I already have a theory about. g35 is the right frame for it -- it is in
the clean part of the ladder (complete 3x3 coarse ring, real climate, from the
contiguous 25-tile block) and it is where the artifact was called clearest.

SECOND DEFECT, INDEPENDENTLY CONFIRMED. The blue specks are the same artifact
already filed as unexplained on the alpine and third-class captures, now seen by a
second observer on g25 and g26. What has been ruled out: undrawn chunks (zero on
every frame), the water subsystem (silent in every log), implicit sea level (water
is implicit only below z=0 per core.h:134, and these tiles bottom well above it),
and the flow plane (the amplifier has no consumer for it). Narrowed but NOT
confirmed to MAT_PERMAFROST (12) or MAT_SNOW (7) from a marginal cold-biome flip,
which fits their appearing on higher and more dissected sites and not on plains.
The colour table is not in C++; it is a generated UE material asset.

WHAT THE SURVEY STILL CANNOT ANSWER. Every frame sits inside a single 15.36 km
tile, so tile-to-tile coherence across a 100-tile world is not in evidence. That
was the original question and it needs more tiles than this machine can generate:
the only torch environment is CPU-only and the GPU is AMD, so CUDA inference is
unavailable rather than merely unconfigured.
