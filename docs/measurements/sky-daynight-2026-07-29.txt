Day/night cycle — measurements, 2026-07-29
==========================================

Every number here was read back from the running engine, not from what was
requested. Ladders are 8 rungs across one simulated day, all in ONE process:
the screenshot noise floor is 0.00% within a session and 1.81% between them
(VoxelGpuVerify.cpp:2074-2084), so rungs from separate runs are not comparable.

Spawn -84480,53760 (tile-coverage centre, real terrain) throughout.
Metric is mean luminance of the sRGB frame, 0-255, over 2560x1440.


1. SOLAR POSITION GATE  —  PASS
-------------------------------
Automation test VoxelEarth.Sky.SolarPosition, headless, -nullrhi.
Within 0.5 deg at four checkpoints: equinox and both solstices at 52 N, plus
70 N at solar midnight (altitude > 0, proving the midnight sun).

In-engine cross-check: noon altitude 60.936 deg measured at 52.4829 N on
day-of-year 171, against 90 - |lat - decl| = 60.96 deg. Delta 0.03 deg.


2. THE THREE DEFECTS THE LADDER FOUND
-------------------------------------
None of these would have been caught by a unit test. All are fixed.

(a) Twilight rendered PURE BLACK.
    Before:  sun -5.1 deg -> mean 0.02, 0.0% non-black
             sun -3.8 deg -> mean 0.02, 0.0% non-black  (byte-identical files)
    After:   55.17 / 98.3% and 83.47 / 96.6%
    Cause chain, verified in engine source rather than guessed:
      SetVisibility(false) -> CreateRenderState_Concurrent skips AddLight
      -> ProcessAtmosphereLightRemoval clears AtmosphereLights[0]
      -> sky pass integrates zero radiance -> black sky
      -> real-time-capture SkyLight captures that black sky as ambient.
    The obvious fix reproduces it: UpdateColorAndBrightness computes
    bValidIntensity as Intensity > 0 unless the light is in EV units, and a
    DirectionalLight is Unitless, so "set intensity to 0" hides it too.

(b) The moon lit terrain twice as orange as the sun.
    kMoonTemperatureK was 4100 K, with a comment correctly arguing for a cool
    Purkinje shift. Colour temperature runs backwards from the word "cool":
    linear RGB at 4100 K is R:B 2.48 against the sun's 5778 K at R:B 1.22.

(c) The darkest night of the year rendered BRIGHTEST.
    The exposure curve capped flat below -2 deg, so it could not distinguish a
    -14 deg midsummer midnight from a -61 deg midwinter one. USkyAtmosphere
    models scattered sunlight only, so its sky is equally dead at both, leaving
    deep night rendered by whatever the moon was doing — and the moon rides
    higher in winter.

4. THE MOVING-SUN COST  —  ANSWERED, AND IT IS FREE
---------------------------------------------------
The open risk was that docs/backlog.md section 0 measured cascades cheap
(shadowGather=0, ~1.03 gathers/frame) with a sun FROZEN SINCE SPAWN. A static
movable directional light lets the engine cache whole-scene shadows; a rotating
one busts that cache every frame. The measurement did not transfer, so it was
re-taken.

tools/voxel-sun-arms.ps1, alternating arms (F,M,F,M — never grouped), 2560x1440,
line flight, 90/120/60 s, identical spawn/seed/route/date, differing only in
-TimeScale. Leg 1 discarded for a cold shader cache (722 hitches against 347).

  arm      p50      p95      max     hitches   sun
  FROZEN   15.18    25.31    69.52     347     alt 37.55, tod 12:00
  MOVING   15.18    25.32    78.19     268     drift 29.4 deg, 12:02 -> 17:23

  p50 identical. p95 identical to 0.01 ms.

  CAVEAT THAT MUST TRAVEL WITH THIS NUMBER: measured with
  voxel.Sky.ShadowUpdateHz = 10, i.e. the light's orientation is re-asserted at
  10 Hz, not every frame. It is THAT CONFIGURATION which is free. An uncapped
  rotating light has not been measured and this result says nothing about it.

  A first attempt at this leg was VOID: it was the first run after a build, the
  editor spent ~5 minutes compiling shaders before the world existed, and the
  480 s wall fired mid-flight. Its editor then outlived the script and the
  one-editor guard correctly refused the remaining three legs rather than
  producing contended numbers. Discard the first run after any build.
