PHASE 5 COLD FILL -- PRE-REGISTERED BEFORE ANY LEG
2026-08-20

Written before voxel.Terrain.RetireQuads has been run at all. Nothing in this
file was chosen after seeing a number.

WHY IT IS PRE-REGISTERED AT ALL
===============================

Cold fill is the headline and IT HAS NEVER MOVED: 35.4 s before this project,
35.6 s after Phase 1. The brick volume was added at zero cost and collected
nothing. Phase 5 is where the saving is supposed to arrive, so the prediction
goes in writing first -- a number chosen afterwards would be indistinguishable
from one chosen to make the phase look successful.

BASELINE
========

  cold fill        80-86 s      (current, this configuration)
  chunks/s          1,074/s
  GPU ceiling      92,000/s     (measured)
  per-chunk mesh   0.969 ms  ->  0.389 ms  = 2.49x, ALREADY MEASURED
                   (mesh 0.810 + pack 0.158  vs  fill 0.227 + pack 0.160)

The 2.49x is a PER-CHUNK MESH-STAGE figure. It is not a fill figure and must
not be quoted as one.

THE PREDICTION
==============

  If cold fill is mesh-stage-bound, a 60% cut to that stage should show as
  AT LEAST A 25% FALL in cold fill: 80-86 s -> roughly 60-65 s.

  If cold fill falls LESS THAN 10%, THE MESH STAGE WAS NOT THE CONSTRAINT.
  The saving lands somewhere invisible, Phase 5 repeats Phase 1's outcome,
  and the finding is "where does cold fill actually go" -- not "how much more
  can be shaved off meshing".

  Between 10% and 25% is a partial result and should be reported as one,
  not rounded toward either edge.

THE DISCRIMINATOR, FIXED NOW SO IT CANNOT BE CHOSEN LATER
=========================================================

chunks/s and cold fill must move TOGETHER.

  both rise/fall together      -> the mesh stage was the constraint. Believe it.
  chunks/s rises, fill holds   -> the constraint MOVED. The next question is
                                  WHERE, not how much more to shave here. A leg
                                  that improves throughput without improving
                                  fill has found a new bottleneck, and that is
                                  a result to report rather than a
                                  disappointment to re-run.
  neither moves                -> the arm did not engage. Check the engagement
                                  counters BEFORE re-running anything: a cvar
                                  reading 1 while the work still happens is the
                                  failure this phase is built to make visible.

WHAT WOULD FALSIFY THE WHOLE FRAMING
====================================

If the engagement counters say the arm engaged, chunks/s rises to near the
92,000/s ceiling, AND cold fill does not move, then cold fill is not
throughput-bound at all and every per-chunk saving in this project has been
measured against the wrong denominator. That is the outcome most worth catching
and the least comfortable, which is why it is written down first.

NOT COVERED
===========

  - Steady-state frame time. Different question, different leg.
  - VRAM (~2,197 MB expected back). Real, separately measured, not a fill term.
  - Water. Untouched by this arm and must stay measurable throughout.
