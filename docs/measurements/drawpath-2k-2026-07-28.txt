============================================================================
1. RESOLUTION IS FREE, WHICH IS THE FINDING THAT MADE THE TARGET REACHABLE
============================================================================

  baseline @ 1600x900   p50 30.61   p95 45.02   hitches 3756
  baseline @ 2560x1440  p50 30.59   p95 44.87   hitches 3747

2.56x the pixels (1.44M -> 3.69M) for ZERO cost, at identical settings. The
renderer is entirely geometry- and submission-bound; nothing in the frame scales
with pixel count at these densities.

That is why the 900p sweeps were worth running at all, and it should be
re-checked rather than assumed after any future change: the moment something
becomes pixel-bound, every 900p result in this file stops transferring.

Density, for context: 29M triangles into 3.69M pixels is 7.8 tri/pixel at 2K
(20 at 900p). A balanced renderer sits nearer 1-2.

============================================================================
2. THE TWO KNOBS ARE INSEPARABLE -- SWEEPING EITHER ALONE FINDS A FALSE FLOOR
============================================================================

voxel.Stream.GPUCullMaxRanges is the draw-range BUDGET. voxel.Stream.
GPUCullMergeGap coalesces ranges separated by fewer than N quads. Swept alone,
each one stalls and says "done":

  GPUCullMaxRanges alone, gap left at its old 4096:
     K=1024  p50 30.61   K=4096  24.11   K=8192  24.06   K=16384  24.35
     -> 16384 is WORSE than 4096. On this evidence alone it would be rejected.

  GPUCullMergeGap alone, K left at 4096:
     gap 4096  23.92   gap 1024  20.49   gap 256  20.64   gap 64  20.59
     -> saturates at 1024, and `ranges` pins at the 4096 cap.

Joint, at 2K:

  gap    K       p50      p95     drawn        ranges
  4096   1024   30.59    44.87    33.1M         1023
  1024   4096   20.51    24.75    14.5M         4095   (cap binding)
  1024   8192   16.12    21.57
   256  16384   15.26    20.95     9.83M        7338
    64  16384   15.15    20.87     9.72M        8002   <- adopted

THE MECHANISM: a coarse gap merges away the very ranges a large budget exists to
keep, so the budget cannot bind and reads as useless. A fine gap PRODUCES many
small ranges, and then the budget is what stops them being merged back. Each
knob is the other's precondition.

GENERAL LESSON, worth carrying: when two parameters gate the same resource, a
one-at-a-time sweep measures each one's effect AT THE OTHER'S WRONG VALUE. The
16384 rejection was a real measurement and a wrong conclusion.

============================================================================
3. OVER-DRAW IS GONE, SO THIS LEVER IS SPENT
============================================================================

  drawnQuads 33,065,957 -> 9,722,222   against visibleQuads 9,710,941

3.4x over-draw -> 1.001x. The cull now submits essentially exactly what is
visible. `ranges` settles at ~8,002, comfortably under the 16384 ceiling, so the
ceiling is no longer the binding constraint -- it is only there so as not to be
one.

There is nothing further for GPUCullMaxRanges or GPUCullMergeGap to recover.
Any further frame-time win must come from LESS GEOMETRY, not from better
selection of the same geometry: occlusion culling (the cull is frustum-only and
frustum already rejects 68% of chunks, but at 7.8 tri/pixel most survivors are
behind something), per-chunk greedy meshing, or cascade reduction.

============================================================================
4. WHAT IS NOT DONE: THE TAIL
============================================================================

p50 clears 60 fps comfortably. p95 is 20.7-20.9 ms = ~48 fps, and max frame is
still 43-47 ms. So 60 fps is the MEDIAN, not the floor, and the worst 5% of
frames miss it.

State this precisely rather than reporting "66 fps": a player feels the tail.
The remaining problem is a different one from the one solved here -- over-draw
is eliminated, so the tail is now about VARIANCE (what makes an occasional frame
3x the median) rather than about volume. Hitch count is already down 3747 -> 8-17
of ~16,000 frames, so whatever remains is rare.

Correctness on both adopted-default legs: holes(final)=0, allocFail=0,
allocFailQuads=0, CoverageVerify green.
