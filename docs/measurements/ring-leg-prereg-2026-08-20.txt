FIRST HONEST RING LEG -- PRE-REGISTERED
2026-08-20, written before the leg runs

The first leg on which `entered`, `hit[1..5]`, `ringDiscordant` and the
per-level gate have ever actually executed. Everything below is a COUNTER;
nothing here is a frame time, so the owner's browser/video load does not touch
any of it.

GATE ZERO -- READ THIS LINE FIRST OR READ NOTHING
=================================================

  JOIN arm rings: shader=1 cpu=1

If shader and cpu disagree, STOP. Every other figure describes a configuration
that does not exist. This is the line whose absence cost three observations
being investigated as independent findings.

MUST NOT MOVE (controls)
========================

  ratio        ~43.5x, band 3.9%   -- six prints held 42.35..44.03
  hash         constant within a leg; prints with different hashes are
               different worlds and are not comparable

A ratio outside that band means the traversal changed, not the rings.

STRONG PREDICTION -- THE BOX FIX
================================

  rayMissedVolume  677,448  ->  ~0

677,448 of 1,354,896 was EXACTLY HALF, and it was the camera sitting on the
corner of a cornered box while the CPU had already moved the origin to the
camera for the ring arm. With the comparator compiled rings-on, the box is
centred on the camera and NO ray should fail the volume test.

If it stays near half, the box and the origin are STILL disagreeing and the
permutation fix did not take. If it lands somewhere in between, a third thing
is involved and nothing downstream should be read.

WHAT IS ACTUALLY BEING MEASURED
===============================

  entered[0]     should be ~= counted rays. Every ray opens ring 0.
  entered[1..5]  THE MEASUREMENT. Unknown, and deliberately not predicted --
                 whether rays get past 128 m at this pose is the open question
                 that "no ray reaches ring 1" was wrongly thought to answer.
  ringDiscordant non-zero IF AND ONLY IF entered[1] > 0. Non-zero with
                 entered[1] == 0 means the classifier fires where no boundary
                 is crossed, which was its pre-registered self-test.
  gate v4        reports at L0; reports at L1+ only where entered[N] > 0. The
                 1.5x bar is a LEVEL-0 number and is not applied above it.

THE DESCENT VERDICT -- AND IT IS NOT INDIFFERENT TO RINGS
=========================================================

The upload-ordering hypothesis is dead (see the hand-back: both walks read the
same bytes through textually identical occupancy reads, so a payload that has
not landed makes BOTH miss and produces no disagreement at all). What remains
is a stepping difference inside one brick.

So the honest prediction is NOT "rings changes nothing":

  IF the mechanism is float conditioning at brick re-entry, rings-on should
  REDUCE descendedAndMissed as a SHARE of lost rays. The centred box puts the
  ulp at ~0.0015 UU against a 0.01 UU nudge, where the cornered box at long
  reach put it at ~0.049 UU -- five times the nudge. That is the exact
  quantity a re-seeding rounding error is sensitive to.

  IF the mechanism is a pure integer stepping difference, the share is
  UNCHANGED and float conditioning is not involved.

  IF descendedAndMissed RISES as a share, neither of us understands it and no
  fix should be attempted until that is explained.

  neverDescended should RISE in ABSOLUTE terms with reach -- more chunks
  crossed is more chances for the chunk-level advance to skip one -- while its
  SHARE stays roughly stable. It is a different defect from the above and it
  is expected to behave differently.

REPORT THE SHARE, NOT THE COUNT. Counts from one print are a snapshot; six
prints established that while ratios held to 3.9% the counts under them moved
2.3x and disagreement 37x. Three prints minimum, bucketed by hash.

WHAT WOULD FALSIFY THE TWO-DEFECT READING
=========================================

If neverDescended and descendedAndMissed move TOGETHER across prints -- both up
or both down by similar factors -- they are one mechanism with two faces and
the "two defects" reading is wrong. The Aug-20 hash leg showed them swapping
dominance (18/42, 2485/5720, 522/22), which is what independence looks like,
but three prints is not a characterised population.
