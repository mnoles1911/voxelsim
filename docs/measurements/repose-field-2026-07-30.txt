THE CORDUROY IS MANUFACTURED IN B3: ONE GLOBAL REPOSE ANGLE PLANES THE FACES
============================================================================
2026-07-30. Thirteenth attempt at the parallel contour-terrace artifact, and the
first to locate WHERE the smoothness is created rather than proving one more
place it cannot be removed.

WHERE THE PREVIOUS FRAMING WAS RIGHT, AND WHERE IT STOPPED SHORT. The
contour-crookedness file proves the client cannot fix this (the carrier alone
measures D = 1.006; the drainage cap forbids exactly the gradients that would
crook a contour; every rotated-frame construction degenerates at 69 km), and its
closing geometric argument -- closed contours require the pit condition -- is
correct as stated. But it proves the wrong thing dispositive: real steep-slope
contours are NOT closed either. Open, parallel-ish contours look natural when
their SPACING varies, because spacing is 100 mm / local grade, and real ground
varies its grade at every scale. So the question is not "how do we close or
crook contours against the cap", it is "why is the local grade of our steep
faces so nearly CONSTANT". That question has a measurable answer, and it is not
"because a heightfield must be smooth".

THE MEASUREMENT. Stage-differenced bake of the g35 exemplar (tile -5,2, seed
20260719, bake_ver 4, no superblock -- statistics only), slope distribution of
the 1.875 m surface over the central 3.84 km window (repro site):

                          p10     p50     p90    frac within +-7% of tan(36)
  B0+B1 carrier+rough    0.263   0.480   0.674        7.7%
  B2d incised            0.328   0.626   1.246       10.7%
  B3 relaxed             0.344   0.706   0.903       19.1%

  B3 moved 40% of cells by >0.1 m (rms 1.33 m, p99 5.3 m), and the histogram
  says exactly what it did: the entire >0.85 tail incision carved is collapsed
  into a spike at 0.70-0.85, i.e. at tan(36 deg) = 0.727. The density JUMPS
  discontinuously at the repose value (4.8% in the 0.65-0.70 bin, 9.1% in
  0.70-0.75, 14.1% in 0.75-0.80). A third of a mountainside within +-10% of one
  angle is a constant-grade surface, and a constant-grade surface voxelises to
  evenly spaced parallel terraces. The corduroy is not an emergent property of
  heightfields; it is the fixed point of a relaxation operator with one global
  threshold.

  (Note B2d's distribution -- broad, heavy-tailed -- is the shape a real steep
  DTM has. The bake CARVES believable variance and then erases it. "Carved then
  erased" is directly visible in the stage diff, which is what stage sinks were
  built for.)

WHY THIS ESCAPES THE GEOMETRIC ARGUMENT. Nothing here perturbs a ramp against
the drainage cap. The fix changes the angle each face is driven TOWARD, in the
bake, before the fill/probe contract is measured. The client is untouched: no
worldgen change, no HLSL mirror, no version bump, no float-ban exposure.
kWorldGenVersion stays where it is; provider_id rolls through bake_ver 5, which
is the sanctioned route for a bake change, and the 52 coarse tiles are consumed
as-is (the bake runs CPU-only in ~2 min/tile on this box).

THE CHANGE (bake_ver 5). thermal.relax accepts a per-cell repose_deg field;
noise.repose_field builds it from two world-anchored components:
  * spatial lithology octaves (160 m and 40 m, +-6 deg RMS) -- patches of
    stronger and weaker rock, so adjacent noses of one ridge finish at
    different angles;
  * elevation-keyed strata bands (30 m and 7.5 m vertical wavelength, +-14 deg
    RMS) -- sub-horizontal bedding, so strong bands hold near-cliff faces and
    keep the relief incision carved into them while weak bands ravel back to
    talus: bench-and-cliff structure, which is exactly the "stop being a smooth
    ramp" escape named in the crookedness file, produced by process rather than
    painted on.
Clamped to [26, 60] deg. Both amplitudes at 0 skip the field and reproduce
bake_ver 4 bit-for-bit. Mass conservation and the steepest-pair stability bound
survive the field (donor-keyed threshold; tested, including numba-vs-numpy).

WHAT THIS DOES NOT FIX, AND THE RANKED REMAINDER. The field only acts where
thermal binds -- steep ground, which is the named blocker. Moderate sub-repose
faces (15-35% grade) keep whatever smoothness the carrier has there. In rank
order of expected value if more is needed:

  1. Strength-modulate INCISION with the same field (hard bands resist
     carving): knickpoints and bench treads at streams, extends the structure
     below the repose regime. Same bake-side machinery, one more consumer.
  2. Client meso band: restore 6.4 m / 12.8 m landform octaves on the fine
     tier under the existing v14/v15 cap. The cap prices gradient, and
     amplitude-per-gradient scales with wavelength, so ~0.5-1 m of relief at
     12.8 m costs what a sub-metre octave costs -- every previous additive
     attempt sat below 3.2 m where the cap forces sub-voxel amplitude. Needs
     the full worldgen/HLSL/version ceremony, hence ranked below the bake
     levers.
  3. Larger horizontal warp (the v16 mechanism at 30-60 m wavelength, metres
     of throw): bends and re-spaces contours structurally without touching
     drainage topology, but dilates every read window and the surface bound;
     and it cannot make benches, only wavier ramps.
