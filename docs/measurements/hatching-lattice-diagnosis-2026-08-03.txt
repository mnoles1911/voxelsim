THE SLOPE HATCHING IS A BIOME-TINT GATE, NOT LOD GEOMETRY
=========================================================
2026-08-03. Site (-38400,-38400), tundra/glacial, alt +1200 m pitch -20 deg,
sun frozen 12:00 03-20. Ladder in bake-out/hatching-ab/ (8 frames + 1:1 crops).

THE ARTIFACT
------------
A repeating chevron / triangle lattice on sloped terrain, visible at distance
and absent up close. Owner confirmed 2026-08-03 that it is NOT desired, and
supplied the decisive clue: "the hatching color and effect goes away when
player is close up. The visual pattern only appears at a distance and typically
on sloped terrain."

BOTH INITIAL HYPOTHESES WERE WRONG, AND COULD NOT HAVE BEEN RIGHT
-----------------------------------------------------------------
I proposed (1) LOD mesh decimation revealing a directional triangulation, and
(2) material / normal MIP aliasing at grazing angles. Neither is possible here:

  * there is no Nanite and no UStaticMesh LOD chain -- coarse levels are
    mip-downsampled voxels RE-MESHED from scratch, never a simplified fine
    mesh, so there is no decimation to reveal;
  * `M_VoxelTerrain` has no albedo texture and no normal map. It is driven by
    vertex colour plus a small biome LUT.

So r.MipMapLoDBias, r.ForceLOD, mip bias and normal-map toggles are all no-ops
on this terrain. Reasoning from the symptom instead of from the material was
the error.

WHAT IT ACTUALLY IS
-------------------
LOD-driven, but through the VERTEX-COLOUR path.

`VoxelQuadVertexFactory.ush:261-265,297` gates the biome tint on

    Decoded.PositionUU.z > (ChunkSurfaceZRelUU - 200.0f)

i.e. a FIXED 2 m band below a surface height sampled ONCE PER CHUNK CENTRE
(`VoxelWorldSubsystem.cpp:10196`). Mirrored on the CPU path in
`VoxelClimateProbe.cpp:154-167`.

A chunk is 3.2 m across at L0 but 51.2 m at L4 and 102.4 m at L5. On a slope
the real surface varies far more than 2 m across a coarse chunk, so most of its
sky-facing faces fall OUTSIDE the band and lose the biome tint entirely. A
horizontal plane cut through a tilted voxel staircase is exactly a
triangle/chevron.

That single mechanism predicts every observation: slope dependence, distance
dependence, the colour change, the chunk-scale cell size, and why the flat
desert vista is nearly clean.

WHAT THIS IS NOT
----------------
Rung 06 is a DIAGNOSTIC, not a proposed fix. Removing that gate is what paints
cave floors grassland green -- the regression the gate was added to prevent
(`VoxelChunkComponent.cpp:192-200`). Both shader edits were reverted and the
file hash verified back to 8685AE04.

One frame per rung. The 06 result is a single settled capture.

NOT TUNDRA-SPECIFIC: the same lattice appears in the rainforest and taiga
vistas in world-maps/seed20260719/vistas/ as green-tread/brown-riser, so any
fix must be checked across biomes.

WHAT A FIX HAS TO DO
--------------------
Keep cave floors untinted while tinting a coarse chunk's sky-facing faces on a
slope. The gate's problem is that it compares a per-voxel z against a
per-CHUNK-CENTRE surface height with a tolerance that does not scale with chunk
size. Candidates, cheapest first:

  1. scale the 2 m band with chunk edge length (3.2 m at L0 -> 102.4 m at L5),
     so the band always spans the surface variation a chunk can contain;
  2. sample the surface height per-COLUMN rather than per-chunk-centre;
  3. use the face normal (sky-facing vs enclosed) rather than a height band,
     which is what the gate is really trying to approximate.

All three need the cave-floor case re-checked, which is what the gate exists
for. Settle-counter discipline applies to every capture judging any of them.
