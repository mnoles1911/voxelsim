The voxel.GI.MaxChunkRefreshesPerFrame sweep that Wave B owed.

WHAT IT WAS FOR. Wave B declined to ship GI on: p50 moved +0.6% (inside noise)
but p95 rose 14.9% and the hitch count TRIPLED, 23.3 -> 74.0. The budget bounds
the re-shade drain that tail comes from, so lowering it was the candidate fix --
and B7 required the sweep to also report the edit-to-relight latency it buys
back, "so the choice is between two named things".

--- HALF ONE: RELIGHT LATENCY. Decisive, and it kills the candidate fix. -------

-VoxelGIRelightAfter=30 (carve at a settled field, time until BOTH the solve
queue and the re-shade queue have drained -- i.e. until the dig is actually
fully relit on screen). One leg per budget, 108 edit bricks each:

  budget   editToFullyRelitMs   peakRefreshQueue
     1     TIMEOUT >30 s (51 of 108 bricks still outstanding)
     2               5,273              711
     4 (default)       240               40
     8                 147               12

STRONGLY NON-LINEAR, AND THE DEFAULT SITS ON THE KNEE. Going 4 -> 2 costs 22x
the relight latency; 4 -> 1 never finishes inside 30 s. A 5.3-second delay
between digging and the hole lighting correctly is not a subtle regression, it
is the kind a player narrates. Going 4 -> 8 buys only 1.6x (240 -> 147 ms).

So the fix Wave B hoped for -- lower the budget, lose the hitches -- is not
available at any price worth paying.

--- HALF TWO: HITCHES. The effect does not reproduce at this anchor. ----------

-VoxelPerfRun=60 -VoxelPerfFlight=static, post-warmup percentiles, 2 legs each:

  config     p50 legs        p95 legs      hitches
  off      12.97 / 13.53   15.55 / 15.80    0 / 0
  on  @4   13.59 / 13.69   15.87 / 16.12    0 / 2
  on  @8   13.20 / 13.62   15.77 / 16.18    0 / 0
  on  @16  13.20           15.85            1

  off same-config spread: p50 0.56 ms, p95 0.25 ms

Every difference BETWEEN budgets is smaller than the spread WITHIN the off
config. More importantly, Wave B's finding is absent: hitches are 0-2 here
against its 23.3 off / 74.0 on, and p50 is 12.97 against its 23.25.

THAT GAP IS THE POINT, NOT A CONTRADICTION. A p50 of 12.97 ms against 23.25 ms
means this is a much cheaper view than the one Wave B measured -- roughly half
the frame cost -- so the re-shade drain never gets deep enough to hitch. The
sweep therefore CANNOT select a budget on hitches at this anchor: the mechanism
it is meant to tune is not present to be tuned.

--- VERDICT -------------------------------------------------------------------

The budget STAYS AT 4, and the sweep does not rescue GI's default.

  * Lowering it is ruled out on evidence, not caution: 22x relight latency at 2,
    no completion at 1.
  * Raising it is unmotivated: 1.6x latency gain, and no hitch improvement
    because there are no hitches here to improve.
  * The tail that justified shipping GI off is anchor-dependent and needs Wave
    B's denser view to reproduce. That is a manual measurement, and it belongs
    with the other frame-cost items a 400 ms-clamped harness cannot take.

voxel.GI.Enabled stays 0.
