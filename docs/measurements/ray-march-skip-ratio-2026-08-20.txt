RAY MARCH SKIP RATIO -- P3-B2a DELIVERABLE
2026-08-20

RESULT
======

  SKIP RATIO  37.4x   (range 36.86 - 38.04, 3.2%)   N = 6 independent worlds

The hierarchical walk (chunks -> bricks -> cells) travels 37.4x fewer steps
than the flat brick walk over the same rays, same frame, same volume.

Plan `docs/ray-marching-plan-2026-08-19.md` assumed 9.19x. Measured is 4.1x
better than assumed.

SCOPE -- what this number covers and what it does NOT
=====================================================

COVERS:
  - Brick pool as the traversal source (voxel.March.Source=1), LEVEL 0 ONLY.
  - Reach 51.2 m, camera-anchored chunk-snapped frame origin.
  - Steps counted per ray by the walk itself; hierarchy vs flat compared
    WITHIN the same frame, so both arms see byte-identical world state.
  - N = 6 legs, six DISTINCT index content hashes.

    CORRECTED 2026-08-20 (later the same day): an earlier version of this
    line read "six different worlds, not six draws of one". THAT WAS NOT
    MEASURED. A content hash is a CHANGE DETECTOR, NOT A DISTANCE METRIC --
    FNV-1a over the index decorrelates completely if a single chunk differs,
    so six distinct hashes prove six distinct states and say NOTHING about
    how far apart they are. Subsequent counters showed total brick entries
    constant to 0.7% across all six, which is what near-identical traversal
    geometry looks like. The legs may be six small perturbations of one
    world. The ratio's 3.2% band should be read as ITS REPEATABILITY OVER
    SIX STATES OF UNKNOWN SEPARATION, not as robustness across worlds.

DOES NOT COVER:
  - The hit/miss mix. UNCHECKED AND IT MATTERS: hierarchy wins most on rays
    that traverse empty space, so a scene where most rays run to reach
    without hitting yields a HIGHER ratio than one where rays stop early.
    CORRECTED: an earlier version of this line said `agreeMiss`/`agreeHit`
    already print on every leg so this costs nothing. WRONG -- those belong
    to the SOURCE compare, which is dark on every camera-anchored frame (the
    gate added to kill the 10x population swing). No leg since that change
    has recorded a hit/miss split at all. A `flatHit`/`hierHit` pair on the
    hier-vs-flat arm was added afterwards to make it measurable.

    NOW MEASURED, and the dependence is LARGER THAN ASSUMED: across eight
    windows `flatHit` ran 46,407 .. 554,789 -- a 12x swing in how much of the
    frame hits terrain at all. The ratio over those same windows held at
    36.25 / 36.25 / 36.70 / 37.04, so it is stable ACROSS that swing, which
    is the reassuring reading. But 37.4x is an average over scenes whose hit
    fraction varies 12x, and it should be quoted with that, not without it.
  - Ring transitions or the 4 km cascade (P3-B2b, planned only, not built).
  - Cone LOD / multi-level descent -- level 0 is the only level walked.
  - Frame time. A step ratio is not a millisecond ratio; the plan's ~2.9x
    frame projection depends on further terms that are not measured here.
  - Correctness. See below. This is a measurement of WORK AVOIDED, and it is
    reported independent of whether every ray lands on the right voxel.

WHY IT IS REPORTED AHEAD OF CORRECTNESS CERTIFICATION
=====================================================

The two are measurably independent, which is not an assumption but the
finding that unblocked this report.

Across the same six legs the raw hierarchy-vs-flat disagreement count ranged
15 .. 10,428 -- a 695x swing, ~2.8 decades, driven by world content alone.
The ratio moved 3.2% across that entire swing:

       ratio     disagree    indexHash
  n1  36.86x         148     0xa46dfa4838e1cf88
  n2  38.04x          41     0xbd185cd2fad7aac8
  n3  37.61x       1,060     0x1896abbb96631ffa
  n4  37.82x          15     0x5a43a6c4afbf673c
  n5  37.82x      10,428     0xe41434175f07f7a3
  n6  37.73x         527     0x4fe521fe25464e1e

If the disagreeing rays materially changed how far the walk travelled, a
695x change in their number would move the ratio. It does not. The residual
defect is LARGE IN COUNT and NEGLIGIBLE IN EFFECT on distance travelled.

So the ratio is not waiting on a gate that measures something it has been
shown to be insensitive to. Correctness remains open and is tracked
separately; it is not a caveat on this number, it is a different number.

MEASUREMENT NOTES
=================

Determinism had to be built before any of this was readable. Two corrections
that changed the answer:

  - The march frame originally borrowed the fluid volume's RecentreTo origin,
    whose hysteresis made byte-identical runs vary 102x. Replaced with a
    camera-anchored chunk-snapped origin; stepsFlat then agreed to 0.017%.

  - The first skip-ratio reading was 66.86x. The comparator rejected it
    (disagree 156,031 against a control refNoise of 6). It was a false
    result SEVEN TIMES BETTER than the one hoped for, and only an instrument
    that states its own verdict caught it. 37.4x is the post-gate number.

A NOTE ON "1,060"
=================

n3 reads exactly 1,060 -- the value treated as "the high mode" for six
rounds of investigation. With N=6 it is visibly a common draw from a skewed
distribution, not a mode. Every earlier statement of the form "high mode is
about 1,000" was a point read from a distribution that had never been
characterised, and the conclusions built on movement away from it were not
supported. Recorded here because the ratio was measured correctly during
exactly the same period the correctness counts were being read wrongly.
