THE BANDING IS THE 3D DENSITY BAND — measured on placed voxels
==============================================================
2026-07-31. Tool: voxel-core/bench/terrainprobe.cpp VXC_PROBE_DUMP_VOXEL +
terrain-service/tools/placed_ab.py. Site -69094,38426, seed 20260719, alpine,
512x512 columns at 1 voxel = 51.2 m across.


1. WHY EVERY EARLIER MEASUREMENT CAME BACK CLEAN
-------------------------------------------------
Every terrain instrument in this tree consumed VXC_PROBE_DUMP, which is
terrainprobe.cpp writing `amp.surfaceMm` -- the continuous HEIGHT FIELD.
band_period.py, riser_height.py, aspect_lock.py, voxel_ladder.py and the whole
probe campaign read that one file format.

Since kWorldGenVersion 12 the client does not render a height field.
Amplifier::stratigraphyAt (amplifier.cpp:2300) tests

    centre <= surfaceMm + D(x, y, z),    |D| <= kDensity3MaxAbsMm = 350 mm

so the solid set is displaced OFF the surface those tools measure, by up to 3.5
voxels. An artifact living in D is not merely hard to see with a heightfield
instrument -- it is structurally invisible to one. That is why roughly twenty
attempts and a full day of measurement kept returning clean data on a game that
stayed visibly banded, and why the owner's inspection of the bake hillshades and
the voxel ladder (continuous / 10 / 20 / 40 / 80 cm) correctly reported all of
them fine. They were fine. They were not what renders.


2. THE INSTRUMENT
------------------
VXC_PROBE_DUMP_VOXEL scans the +/-350 mm band DOWNWARD per column and writes the
top solid voxel's top face, i.e. what the mesher sees, and writes it twice:

    <path>        col.d3 as built           -- what ships
    <path>.nod3   col.d3 default-constructed -- the term off

The second is an EXACT ablation with no constant touched and no version bump:
density3ColumnDisplacementMm returns 0 identically when gateQ == 0 (density3.h
section 0), so a zeroed Density3Column is the term switched off bit-exactly.
This matters because density3.h deliberately refuses a real off switch -- a flag
producing v11 geometry under a v12 stamp is a desync waiting to happen. Ablating
in the PROBE has neither problem and needs no rebuild of anything shipped.

It also means the two earlier "bedding is not the cause" negatives can now be
explained rather than merely doubted: both ablated kBeddingAmpMm (the 2D term,
120 mm) and left kBedding3AmpMm at 350, because
static_assert(kBeddingAmpMm > 0 && kBedding3AmpMm > 0) and the budget assert
kBedding3MaxAbsMm == kDensity3MaxAbsMm block zeroing the 3D one. The larger,
volumetric, instrument-invisible term was never under test.


3. THE RESULT
--------------
At -69094,38426, over 262,144 columns:

    d3 slope+lithology gate open        262144 / 262144   (100.00%)
    top solid voxel MOVED by the band   232282 / 262144   ( 88.61%)
    displacement                        -300 .. +300 mm, mean|d| 212 mm, std 211
    relief                              45.2 m with, 45.0 m without

Side by side under identical light (placed-ab-69094.png), the band-off panel is
clean, irregular, natural-looking rock. The band-on panel is the owner's
artifact exactly as described: parallel, evenly spaced, sharing direction,
running diagonally across the slope. Same ground, same light, one term.

SPECTRUM OF THE DISPLACEMENT FIELD (with_d3 - no_d3), Hann-windowed 2D FFT:

    dominant wavelength        10.04 m   (apparent on-ground spacing)
    stripes run at             78.7 deg
    variance by wavelength band
        0.4 - 0.8 m     4.9%
        0.8 - 1.6 m    19.2%
        1.6 - 3.2 m    40.9%    <-- 60.1% combined
        3.2 - 6.4 m     8.3%
        6.4 - 25.6 m   18.0%

60% of the variance falls in 0.8-3.2 m, which is detail_bedding.h's documented
bed thickness range verbatim. The mechanism is not inferred from the picture; it
is pinned to a specific constant by the spectrum. The 10 m dominant APPARENT
wavelength is the bed thickness projected onto the slope by the dip -- a bed of
fixed thickness outcrops wider the shallower it cuts the face, which is also why
the spacing looks so regular over a uniform slope.


4. WHY IT LOOKS THE WAY IT DOES, TERM BY TERM
----------------------------------------------
Every property the owner reported falls out of the term's own design:

  parallel                 bedding planes are parallel by construction
  evenly spaced            bed thickness 800-3200 mm
  jumps several voxels     |D| <= 350 mm = 3.5 voxels; measured p5/p95 +/-300
  only on steep ground     slope gate 600-800 mm/m, lithology gate bare rock
  diagonal, direction
    flips between areas    regional strike/dip hashed from an 819.2 m lattice
                           off a table of 16 integer direction pairs, so strike
                           is CONSTANT within a domain and steps at its edges

The contrast curve (kDensity3SharpenPasses = 3) is what makes the contacts hard
rather than sinusoidal. density3.h's own comment predicted the visual outcome
and approved of it: "it turns a sine-ish corrugation into a stack of ledges."
That is precisely the artifact. The term is doing what it was written to do; the
disagreement is with the specification, not the implementation.

density3.h also anticipates the objection and dismisses it, wrongly:

    "A flat region in D means dD/dz = 0, which is an exactly VERTICAL face -- on
     a cliff that is a feature, not a terrace. Terracing is flatness in the
     SURFACE HEIGHT field; this is flatness in a vertical displacement, and the
     two are perpendicular."

The argument is sound about a vertical face and irrelevant to the observed
geometry. On a 60-90% grade the face is not vertical, so a periodic vertical
displacement of the solid set writes a periodic band ACROSS the slope -- and at
88.61% of columns moved, it is not decorating the surface, it IS the surface.


5. WHAT ACTUALLY SOLVED IT -- THE OWNER'S OWN VERDICT
------------------------------------------------------
Recorded because it generalises past this artifact. What broke the case open was
not a statistic. It was being shown bake maps and top-down renders of the SAME
terrain area at successive stages of the pipeline, so the owner could narrow down
where the artifact was being introduced.

The numbers could not settle it, by construction: every metric in the harness was
computed on the height field while the artifact lived in voxel placement, so
improving those metrics was indistinguishable from progress. The owner's eye was
the only instrument that could see the artifact at all. The fast path was not a
better statistic -- it was giving that instrument a controlled comparison at each
stage.

The ladder, each panel the same ground under the same light:

    bake hillshade, 3 bake variants          -> "all three look fine"
    continuous client surface                -> "looks great"
    voxel 10 cm, LOD 20 / 40 / 80 cm         -> "all look great"
    placed voxels, 3D band OFF vs ON         -> "exactly what we don't want"

Each "this looks fine" eliminated an entire stage in one sentence. Three sentences
eliminated the whole generator and the whole voxelisation ladder -- which no
amount of measurement had managed in twenty attempts, because the measurements
were all of the stages that were already fine.

THE RULE, for the next artifact: when the owner reports something visual and the
metrics disagree, the owner is right and the metric is measuring the wrong thing.
Do not iterate on the metric. Build the A/B ladder -- same ground, same light,
same framing, native resolution with no downsampling, exactly one thing varying
per panel -- and prefer ablating in the PROBE over ablating in shipped constants,
because that needs no rebuild, no version bump, and cannot leave a flag set.


6. THE FIX, AND HOW IT WAS VERIFIED (worldgen v20, same day)
-------------------------------------------------------------
Commit 1 of 2: the term is no longer applied. Amplifier::stratigraphyAt tests
`centre <= surfaceMm` again and Amplifier::column leaves ColumnSample::d3
default-constructed; worldgen.ush mirrors both, and stratigraphyAt/materialAt
there drop the four parameters (seed, xMm, yMm, columnGateQ) that existed only
to evaluate D. Bounds are deliberately UNCHANGED and still widened by
kDensity3MaxAbsMm -- conservative is never unsound, and the tightening is
commit 2 so the geometry change and the bound change can be reverted apart.

THE DECISIVE CHECK. The v20 placed-voxel dump at -69094,38426 is BIT-IDENTICAL
to the `.nod3` panel of the A/B above -- the one the owner inspected and
approved -- and differs from the shipped v19 geometry on 232,282 of 262,144
columns. So the change does exactly what the ablation predicted and nothing
else. That comparison is the whole point of having ablated in the probe first:
the prediction was made before the code was touched, and could then be checked
against it rather than merely described.

  vxc_tests            381 pass, 0 fail (2 golden digests regenerated:
                       amplifier_deep_column 0x09D0..741D -> 0xB030..1779,
                       coarsegen 0xAD39..FCBF -> 0x946C..DBAF)
  overhang scan        0 overhung columns of 185,850 (was ~1.97% of gated)
  surface bound sweep  1320 footprints, 1,775,101 dense samples, VIOLATIONS=0,
                       slack min 5587 mm
  vxc_gpu              PASS, bit-exact CPU vs GPU over 25,728 columns /
                       903,168 CELLS / 12,137 quads, 8 regions including
                       `density-band-cliff`. Cell-level (not column-level)
                       comparison is what makes this meaningful for a term that
                       hashed per voxel.
  vxc_bench            slope gate open on 0/409,600 columns; 0 of 11.6M visited
                       voxels inside an open band; 0.0 ns/gated voxel
  prebuilt SPIR-V      respun, exactly ONE kernel changed (VoxelizeMain), which
                       is the only kernel the term lived in

TWO GUARDS BEHAVED WELL AND ARE WORTH THE RECORD, because this project has been
burned by guards that did not.

  * test_amplifier.cpp's non-vacuity assertion -- "otherwise this test would
    keep passing after someone disabled the term" -- is what FAILED when the
    term was disabled. It was right, it did its job, and it was removed only
    because the removal is now intentional. It has been replaced by its inverse
    (gated == 0, displacedTop == 0, and a new amplifier_world_is_a_heightfield
    test asserting no column anywhere is overhung), so reintroducing a
    displacement fails a test rather than passing silently.
  * compile-shaders.ps1's staleness warning correctly named
    worldgen.VoxelizeMain.spv as stale before -UpdatePrebuilt was passed.

ONE GUARD IS NARROWER THAN IT LOOKS, and this was checked rather than assumed.
worldgen.ush's version-skew #error is inside `#ifdef VXC_UE`, so it fires only
on the Unreal compile path, where ModifyCompilationEnvironment supplies
VXC_WORLDGEN_VERSION_CPP. Deliberately setting VXC_WORLDGEN_VERSION_USH back to
19 against a CPP value of 20 and running tools/compile-shaders.ps1 compiled
CLEANLY. That is correct scoping -- standalone dxc has nothing to compare
against -- but it means the standalone path is covered by the staleness warning
alone, not by the #error. Passing -D VXC_WORLDGEN_VERSION_CPP=<n> parsed out of
core.h would make the guard live there too; not done here, flagged.

NOT VERIFIED HERE: NVIDIA digest parity. vxc_gpu passed on an AMD Radeon RX
7800 XT, which is the only device on this machine. The AMD-vs-NVIDIA gate is the
one that caught the truncDiv/floorDiv divergence historically and it has not
been run for v20.

THE OWNER'S VERDICT, which is the one that closes this. Judging a LIVE
interactive session -- editor at the cliff site (-69094,38426), camera 250 m,
pitch -20 deg, sun frozen 12:00 03-20, real diffusion tiles (tile grid, 25
tiles, no synthetic fallback), v20 build: "YES THIS LOOKS GOOD. PASS".

TWO PROCESS FAILURES ON THE WAY THERE, recorded because both nearly reopened a
closed investigation:

  * THE FIRST TWO CAPTURES WERE OF THE WRONG WORLD. This repo has no
    tile-cache/, so DefaultGame.ini's DefaultTileDir resolves to nothing and the
    engine falls back to the synthetic sampler -- it says so in the log, and
    both the capture script's header and DefaultGame.ini's own comment warn that
    the fallback is "a plausible-looking fake world". Captures here MUST pass
    -VoxelTileDir explicitly until the cache is present (open task #15), and the
    log line to check is `VoxelClimateProbe: tile grid, tiles=N` -- if it reads
    SYNTHETIC, throw the capture away.
  * I READ A CAPTURE AS "STILL BANDED" AND IT WAS NOT. The owner rejected that
    reading. I had pattern-matched regular repeating structure to the artifact
    without checking it against what they had actually been describing, and was
    already building a probe for the LOD path to chase it. The rule now, in the
    owner's words, is "I should be the judge of any recent screenshots": produce
    the capture and state the conditions that make it trustworthy -- settle
    counters, tile source, sun pins, pose, what varies between panels -- and
    stop there. The conditions are mine to verify; the verdict is theirs.


7. WHAT IS NOT ESTABLISHED
---------------------------
  * The gate rate at this site is 100% of columns against the design's measured
    6.52% world-average over 25 real diffusion tiles. This is a 51.2 m window
    chosen for being steep, so it is a worst case, not a typical one. Three
    other sites probed the same day (+900, +1800, +2700 m) read 0.00%. How much
    of the world looks like the right-hand panel has NOT been measured; the
    census that would answer it is a wider stride than terrainprobe's fixed
    1-voxel dump currently allows.
  * This is a measurement of the CPU path. worldgen.ush carries its own copies
    of every constant, so the GPU mirror has not been shown to agree here --
    though a disagreement would be a separate and worse bug.
  * No fix is proposed or tested. kDensity3MaxAbsMm, kDensity3SharpenPasses and
    the bed thickness are all flagged PROVISIONAL in their own source; which of
    them to move, and whether the term survives at all, is the next decision.
    Note the term buys an overhang on ~1.97% of gated columns for this.
