TWO EMPTY BIOME GATES: SAVANNA AND DESERT AT worldgen v21 -> v22
=================================================================
2026-08-01. Constants: kBiomeSeasonalHighU8 (removed), kBiomePrecipSeasonalHighU8
(new), kBiomeTempHotU8 (re-examined, NOT moved). All in
voxel-core/include/voxelcore/biome.h + its worldgen.ush mirror.

Ground truth: WorldClim 2.1 10-arc-minute bio_1/4/12/15 and etopo_10m, cropped
h//6 .. h-h//6 (+/-60 deg latitude) with values < -30000 treated as nodata --
the same crop and nodata rule _compute_map_stats uses, so these numbers describe
exactly the distribution the conditioning statistics were built from. 437,571
land pixels; every "% of land" below is cos(latitude) area-weighted.
Game world: the coarse model at seed 20260719, 96x96 coarse cells (7.68 km each).


1. WHAT WAS WRONG
------------------
SAVANNA had never been produced. Not rarely -- never. The v8..v21 gate was

    tempU8 >= kBiomeTempWarmU8 (18 C)  AND  seasonalityU8 >= 1500 (bio_4)

and those two conditions do not co-occur on Earth. The maximum bio_4 anywhere
with bio_1 >= 18 C is 1084, against a threshold of 1500: ZERO of 437,571 land
pixels satisfy the gate. MAT_SAVANNA_GRASS has therefore never been placed by
any build, and every warm semi-arid column fell through to GRASSLAND.

It is close to mutually exclusive by construction rather than by accident. The
warm band IS the tropics, and what makes a place tropical is that its
temperature barely moves through the year -- so asking for high TEMPERATURE
seasonality inside the warm band asks for a contradiction.

DESERT was also at 0.00% of land in the v21 census, but for a completely
different reason, and the distinction is the main point of this file. See 4.


2. NO bio_4 THRESHOLD FIXES SAVANNA -- IT IS THE WRONG VARIABLE
-----------------------------------------------------------------
Sweeping the bio_4 threshold down until it admits a plausible share does not
produce savanna, it produces mislabelled temperate land. Inside the eligible
window (bio_1 >= 18 C and 400 <= bio_12 < 1600, which is 22.47% of Earth land
and is the ceiling on any savanna rule):

    rule            share of land   of which inside |lat| < 25
    ------------------------------------------------------------
    window itself       22.47%              87.2%     <- base rate
    bio_4  >= 200       12.49%              77.0%     <- DEPLETED
    bio_4  >= 300        7.42%              62.6%     <- worse
    bio_15 >=  70       16.27%              93.4%     <- enriched
    bio_15 >=  90       10.77%              93.4%

A bio_4 rule is ANTI-selective for the tropics: it takes a SMALLER tropical
fraction than the window it draws from. On named sites, bio_4 >= 200 calls
Houston, Brisbane, Miami, Durban and Seville savanna while rejecting the
Serengeti, the Cerrado and Tsavo.

The variable that separates savanna from both rainforest and temperate woodland
is a DRY SEASON, which is a precipitation phenomenon. bio_15 (CV of monthly
precipitation) was already in the wire format and already uploaded to the GPU:

  site                bio_1  bio_12  bio_4  bio_15   in window  b15>=70  b4>=200
  Niamey, Sahel        29.3     497    303     137      Y          Y        Y
  Serengeti            19.8     837     95      65      Y          .        .
  Cerrado, Brasilia    21.1    1460    130      83      Y          Y        .
  Katherine, N Aus     27.2     982    285     114      Y          Y        Y
  Zambia miombo        20.2    1034    248     113      Y          Y        Y
  Deccan, India        27.0     794    309     108      Y          Y        Y
  Tsavo, Kenya         24.6    1000    146      72      Y          Y        .
  Burkina Faso         27.8     807    223     117      Y          Y        Y
  ---- warm, in the same precipitation window, NOT savanna ----
  Houston, TX          20.9    1209    639      22      Y          .        Y
  Brisbane, AU         20.2    1218    395      46      Y          .        Y
  Miami, FL            24.5    1498    318      54      Y          .        Y
  Durban, ZA           20.8    1010    267      41      Y          .        Y
  Seville, ES          18.4     536    595      67      Y          .        Y

bio_15 >= 70 accepts 8 of 9 savanna sites (the Serengeti, at 65, is the miss)
and rejects all 5 in-window negatives. bio_4 >= 200 gets 5 wrong of 13.


3. THE NEW THRESHOLD IS DERIVED TWICE AND BOTH ROUTES GIVE 70%
----------------------------------------------------------------
PHYSICAL. A year with d months of no rain and the rest uniform has
CV = sqrt(d / (12 - d)). Four dry months -- a third of the year, the usual
marked-dry-season line for tropical savanna -- is sqrt(4/8) = 70.7%.

    d dry months   CV      SAVANNA, % of Earth land
       2          44.7%          20.60%
       3          57.7%          18.75%
       4          70.7%          16.13%     <- shipped
       5          84.5%          12.52%
       6         100.0%           8.32%

EMPIRICAL. 70% puts SAVANNA at 15.57% of Earth land in the full classifier
(the 16.13% above is the gate in isolation; the treeline and cold gates take the
rest), against the ~15.6% that tropical/subtropical grassland-savanna-shrubland
actually covers. The neighbours bracket it and are both worse: d=3 starts
admitting Mediterranean Seville, d=6 drops the Cerrado.

kBiomePrecipSeasonalHighU8 = climatePrecipVarU8FromDeciPct(700) = u8 89, which
decodes back to 69.8% -- one u8 step is 0.78%, so the quantization is not doing
anything interesting here.

EARTH CENSUS, % of land area, no slope gate, elevation from etopo:

    biome              v21 (bio_4 1500)   v22 (bio_15 70%)
    BEACH                    5.90%             5.90%
    GRASSLAND               30.93%            25.25%
    TEMPERATE_FOREST        21.88%            11.99%
    RAINFOREST              12.88%            12.88%
    DESERT                   9.50%             9.50%
    SAVANNA                  0.00%            15.57%
    TAIGA                    9.35%             9.35%
    TUNDRA_ALPINE            9.56%             9.56%


4. DESERT IS A DIFFERENT BUG AND kBiomeTempHotU8 WAS NOT MOVED
----------------------------------------------------------------
The v21 census showed DESERT at 0.00% of land while 16.74% of that land is arid
(bio_12 < 400). The obvious reading -- "the 24 C gate is too high, lower it" --
is wrong, and the measurement says so twice.

On EARTH, 24 C is already the right answer:

    temp_hot   DESERT, % of Earth land
      18 C          18.17%
      20 C          16.09%
      22 C          13.04%
      24 C           9.50%     <- Earth is ~8.6%
      26 C           6.30%

Every lower value overshoots hard; at 18 C half the world's dry TEMPERATE land
is sand.

In the CENSUS WORLD there is no value that works at all. Land temperature over
3,464 land cells:

    bio_1 >= 14 C : 51.93% of land        p1   -0.5 C
    bio_1 >= 16 C : 36.23%                p5    3.1
    bio_1 >= 18 C : 19.75%                p50  14.3
    bio_1 >= 20 C :  7.04%                p95  20.6
    bio_1 >= 22 C :  1.27%                p99  22.1
    bio_1 >= 24 C :  0.06%                max  24.9   <- the WHOLE world's max
    bio_1 >= 26 C :  0.00%

Earth land, same crop: p5 -3.8, p50 17.3, p95 27.6, max 31.0. The coarse model's
land is about 7 C short at the warm end and its maximum barely clears the gate.
Consequently:

    if temp_hot were   14 C  16 C  18 C  20 C  22 C  24 C
    census DESERT     5.60% 3.67% 1.79% 0.35% 0.03% 0.00%

Nothing reaches Earth's 8.6% even at 14 C -- a threshold that would put Madrid
and Paris in the "hot" band. THE GATE IS CORRECT AND THE WORLD IS WRONG. The
blocker is the coarse model's compressed climate tails, which is the same
mechanism already recorded for elevation in
conditioning-stats-real-climate-2026-08-01.txt (finding 1) and for the cold end
of temperature (finding 3). Fixing it belongs to the model, not to biome.h.


5. THE CENSUS, BEFORE AND AFTER, ON IDENTICAL DATA
----------------------------------------------------
Same generated coarse world, classified both ways in one run, so this is a
controlled comparison rather than two separate generations. Seed 20260719,
96x96 coarse cells, orographic ON. The v21 column reproduces the previously
recorded census exactly, which is what validates the harness.

    biome              v21 (bio_4)   v22 (bio_15)
    BEACH                   6.15%         6.15%
    GRASSLAND              47.01%        44.68%
    TEMPERATE_FOREST       31.94%        31.21%
    RAINFOREST              4.39%         4.39%
    DESERT                  0.00%         0.00%   <-- still zero, see 4
    SAVANNA                 0.00%         3.06%
    TAIGA                   2.44%         2.44%
    TUNDRA_ALPINE           8.07%         8.07%

    land precip p5/p50/p95   244 / 700 / 2470 mm    (16.74% under 400)
    land temp   p5/p95       3.3 / 20.7 C           (0.08% at or above 24 C)
    land bio_15 p5/p50/p95   16 / 50 / 94 %         (21.13% over the 70% gate)
    land bio_4  p5/p50/p95   480 / 752 / 1143       (0.00% over the old 1500)

Orographic OFF is within 0.2 points on every row; the gate change is not
sensitive to Wave 1b.

SAVANNA IS NOW REACHABLE, WHICH IS THE FIX. 3.06% is well short of Earth's
15.6%, and the shortfall decomposes into two separate deficits:

    quantity                          census world    Earth (+/-60)
    warm AND 400-1600 mm (eligible)      13.28%          22.47%
    bio_15 >= 70% over all land          21.22%          44.60%
    bio_15 >= 70% INSIDE the window      24.5%           72.4%
    bio_15 >= 70% OUTSIDE the window      --             36.5%

The first two are the tail compression of section 4 again. The THIRD is a
different and previously unrecorded defect: on Earth a warm 400-1600 mm place is
usually monsoonal, so bio_15 >= 70% is TWICE as likely inside the window
(72.4%) as outside it (36.5%). In the census world it is 24.5% inside against
21.22% over all land -- essentially independent, which is what an independently
sampled channel gives you.
The model reproduces each channel's marginal and not their joint -- which is
expected, since the channels are independent noise fields quantile-matched one
at a time, but it had not been measured before. It is the reason a correct gate
still under-produces savanna, and it is the thing to attack next if savanna
share matters.


6. WHAT SHIPPED
----------------
kWorldGenVersion 21 -> 22 (core.h, with the changelog entry).
VXC_WORLDGEN_VERSION_USH 21 -> 22 (worldgen.ush).
kBiomeSeasonalHighU8 removed; kBiomePrecipSeasonalHighU8 = 89 added, in both the
header and the HLSL mirror (mirror value taken from vxc_dump_biome_constants,
not hand-computed).
classifyBiome's third argument changed channel, bio_4 -> bio_15; all three
non-test call sites moved (Amplifier::column, worldgen.ush ColumnMain,
climateprobe.cpp). The shader now blends bits 24-31 of the packed climate word
instead of bits 8-15; the packing and the wire format are UNCHANGED, so no tile
needs regenerating and provider_id does not roll.
terrain-service/tools/world_map.py and water_viewer.py follow the header (they
parse it, so a stale copy would have exited loudly rather than drawn a wrong
map) and now read the coarse model's "Precip CV" channel.
voxel-core/shaders/prebuilt/worldgen.ColumnMain.spv respun; the other six
kernels are byte-identical.

gpu_harness.cpp gained a ninth fixture, "savanna-boundary" -- see below.

TESTS. vxc_tests: 361 passed, 0 failed (360 before; one test added,
biome_savanna_gate_is_reachable_from_real_climates, which pins the site table in
section 2 so a gate no real climate satisfies fails in CI).
GOLDEN(biome_map) re-pinned 0x7D36AFFD6B9DE5C5 -> 0xD7E49028948294F5. Only the
SAVANNA cells of that sweep move.

vxc_gpu: PASS, bit-exact CPU/GPU over 29,824 columns / 1,067,008 cells /
14,642 quads on an AMD Radeon RX 7800 XT.

THE FIRST vxc_gpu RUN OF THIS CHANGE WAS WORTHLESS AND ALMOST GOT BELIEVED. Its
build had failed on missing Vulkan headers while a piped exit status hid it, so
a STALE v21 binary ran against the freshly respun v22 SPIR-V -- exactly the
stale-voxelcore.lib shape VoxelGpuVerify.cpp warns about. It reported 0
mismatches over 25,728 columns and looked like a pass.

Rebuilt properly it also reported 0 mismatches, and that second result exposed
the real problem: NONE of the eight existing fixtures reaches the changed gate.
The accidental v21-CPU-against-v22-GPU comparison is the proof -- if any fixture
had exercised the savanna branch, the two versions would have disagreed there.
Over every fixture the synthetic sampler is either too cool or too wet, so
`warm && seasonal` is false everywhere and clPrecipVar could have been shifted
by the wrong amount with every region still passing. This is the failure the
harness's own density-band-cliff comment describes: "a gate that closes
everywhere in the fixture is indistinguishable from a term that is not mirrored
at all."

So a fixture was added by SEARCH, the same way that one was. "savanna-boundary"
sits at voxel (-249632, 1151968), 64x64 at 30 m pitch, on a tile-pixel CORNER
where the 2x2 climate block straddles the CV threshold while all four corners
stay warm and inside the savanna precipitation window. It contains 2,133
SAVANNA columns against 1,963 TEMPERATE_FOREST, with the boundary between them
set by the faded-bilinear blend of the byte that moved -- so a wrong shift, a
wrong blend or a wrong threshold relocates it into column mismatches. Climate
there (u8): temp 207, precip 17, bio_15 89, against gates 185 / 9..34 / 89.
Relief 2.7 m, 5 bricks of z, 2,636 quads. It passes bit-exact.

kExpectedCpuDigest (VoxelGpuVerify.cpp) DID NOT CHANGE, and that is a finding
rather than a pass. Re-measured with a standalone mirror of the CPU-side fold,
validated by reproducing the v21 value 0xb2b5d2f1044caa35 byte for byte before
the change was applied; it prints the same value after. The reason is that
NEITHER DIGEST FIXTURE CAN REACH THE CHANGED GATE: across all 8,192 columns of
the two regions the synthetic sampler gives temperature u8 [66, 114] against
kBiomeTempWarmU8 = 185 and precipitation u8 [47, 66] against
kBiomePrecipModU8 = 34, so zero columns are both warm and inside the savanna
window -- old gate fires 0 times, new gate fires 0 times. Only
kExpectedCpuDigestWorldGenVersion moved, 21 -> 22.


7. WHAT IS STILL UNKNOWN
-------------------------
* Nobody has SEEN savanna. Every number here is a classifier census; no
  screenshot of MAT_SAVANNA_GRASS on real ground exists, and per the standing
  rule the owner judges screenshots, not censuses. The 3.06% is spread across a
  768 km window, so finding a patch worth capturing needs world_map.py first.
* The 24.5% vs 69.3% joint deficit in section 5 is measured but not diagnosed.
  It could be the quantile-matching, the diffusion model's own smoothing, or the
  orographic pass; nothing here separates them.
* Whether 70% is right AT 30 m. Every threshold here was derived against 10-arcmin
  WorldClim (~18 km) and the game classifies per 30 m pixel off tiles whose
  climate came through the same coarse channels. A CV computed at 18 km and a CV
  interpolated to 30 m are not the same statistic, and no measurement here
  bridges that.
* VoxelGpuVerify's OWN two fixtures still cannot reach the savanna gate
  (section 6), so voxel.GPU.VerifyRegion -- the Unreal-side gate, which is a
  different code path from vxc_gpu -- does not cover the changed branch. vxc_gpu
  now does. Closing it needs a savanna-bearing region added to
  kBandOnlyRegions (NOT to kRegions, which would move the pinned digest);
  the origin found here is on the synthetic sampler and transfers directly.
* kBiomeTreelineBaseMm is still TUNED, not derived, and TUNDRA_ALPINE at 8.07%
  of census land sits on it. Untouched here.
* ClimateSample::seasonality (bio_4) is now read by NOTHING in worldgen. It stays
  in the wire format and is still generated, blended and tested; it just has no
  consumer. Removing it would roll the tile format, which is not worth doing for
  one byte, but it should not be mistaken for live data.
