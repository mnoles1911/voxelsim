THE MICRO GRADIENT CAP: 1.5 -> 1.2 at worldgen v21
====================================================
2026-08-01. Constant: kMicroGradCapKQ10 (amplifier.cpp). Site: g35 repro
-69094,38426 for drainage, -67500,40000 (gentle) for terracing. Seed 20260719.
OWNER-CONFIRMED in a live session: terrain passes on every item of the test
checklist, including the gentle-ground item this change was actually about.


1. WHY IT COULD ONLY BE JUDGED NOW
-----------------------------------
v19 raised the micro pool's cap above 1.0 so the 400/200 mm octaves could break
voxel-quantisation terracing, and chose 1.5 over 1.2 while the owner's rejected
banding was still unexplained. Band width was therefore being bought at whatever
drainage it cost. v20 identified that banding as the 3D density band -- which no
setting of this constant could ever have affected. With the confound gone the
two columns can be traded against each other honestly, which is the whole reason
this was sequenced AFTER v20 rather than alongside it.


2. THE TWO MEASUREMENTS, AND THEY DISAGREE ABOUT WHICH WAY TO GO
------------------------------------------------------------------
DRAINAGE, amplified surface at the alpine site (carrier control: 0 sinks,
0.0% stranded):

    k=0.5    1 sink    0.4% stranded
    k=1.0    5 sinks   0.4% stranded
    k=1.2    9 sinks   1.2% stranded   <-- shipped
    k=1.5   17 sinks   3.5% stranded

The knee is at 1.0, NOT at 0.5. Below 1.0 there are four sinks left to win and
no stranded area at all, while a cap at or below 1.0 provably cannot disturb a
quantisation band at any slope -- the required detail gradient is 2x the
carrier's, identically, at every slope. v14's factor-of-four margin was never
paying for itself, so the agreed "revert to 0.5" was dropped on evidence.

TERRACING, gentle site, amplified:

                              k=1.2        k=1.5
    mean run                  3.35 vox     3.04 vox
    median / p90 / p99        2 / 7 / 15   2 / 6 / 13
    area in flat patches
      >= 4 m^2                39.4%        15.4%

So 1.2 costs 2.6x the flat-plateau area. Both numbers are on the table because
they point opposite ways: drainage says lower, terracing says higher.


3. THE METRIC THAT NEARLY HID IT
----------------------------------
Read the run-length rows again. Mean 3.35 vs 3.04 voxels, medians identical,
p90 within one voxel -- by that statistic the two settings are the same surface.
By plateau area they differ 2.6-fold.

The run-length distribution is dominated by thousands of tiny runs, so it
averages the artifact away; plateau area measures the thing the eye locks onto,
which is a big flat shelf. On the strength of the run-length rows alone the
honest prediction was "you will not be able to tell them apart", and that
prediction was stated to the owner and was WRONG.

This is the same failure that cost a full day on the banding artifact: a metric
that is real, correctly computed, and measuring the wrong quantity. It is worth
stating as a rule -- for a terracing artifact, measure the AREA IN FLAT PATCHES,
not the length of runs. One is what the eye reads and the other is what the
histogram is made of.


4. THE DECISION
----------------
Shipped 1.2. The drainage win is halved sinks and threefold less stranded area;
the terracing cost is 39.4% of gentle ground in >= 4 m^2 flat patches against
15.4%. The owner tested the shipped build in a live session against an explicit
checklist -- steep bare rock for the v20 banding regression, GENTLE GROUND for
this trade, channel connectivity for the fine tier, general believability -- and
passed it on all of them.

That verdict is what settles it, not the plateau number: 39.4% is worse than
15.4% and both are far better than an uncapped micro pool, and the only question
that ever mattered was whether the difference is visible at play distance. It is
not. Had it been, k=1.35 was the next thing to sweep.

RESIDUAL, stated because the numbers above are not zero: 9 interior sinks and
1.2% stranded area remain against a carrier that has neither. Detail still
strands some drainage; it strands a third of what v19 did.
