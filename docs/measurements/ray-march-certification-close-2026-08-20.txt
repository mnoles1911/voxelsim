HIER-VS-FLAT CERTIFICATION: CLOSED
2026-08-20

THE RESULT
==========

The question "do the two walks agree" WAS ILL-POSED, and an arbiter is what
showed that -- not another hypothesis about why they disagree.

A direct, non-accumulating slab test (O(1) ulp against a walk's O(N)) was
asked, per disagreeing ray, which candidate the ray genuinely enters first,
with solidity taken from the pool rather than from either walk. Four legs:

  CONTESTED (both walks offered a candidate) -- ~90% of the population
      flat-correct 34-40%   hier-correct 35-39%   tie 14-21%

  A coin flip, with a fifth genuinely undecidable. At a cell plane the answer
  is ambiguous in float. NEITHER WALK IS RIGHT. This population is the
  resolution limit of float t and is NOT A DEFECT.

  UNCONTESTED (exactly one walk offered a candidate at all)

  CORRECTED 2026-08-20, SAME DAY. First reported as flat-only 13-26 against
  hier-only 3-9, "~20 rays per leg". THOSE COUNTS WERE SUPPRESSED by the
  sentinel bug (below) and by the arbiter's own degenerate-span rejection,
  both of which discard uncontested rays before they are counted. After the
  sentinel fix:

      n2   flat-only 3,870   hier-only 698    asymmetry  5.5x
      n3   flat-only   469   hier-only   8    asymmetry 58.6x
      n1   flat-only    16   -- NO VERDICT, 44% ill-posed, a floor

  CORRECTED AGAIN, same day, and this time the ASYMMETRY itself did not
  survive. With all three of the arbiter's strict boundary comparisons given
  the derived tolerance, the one leg with statistical weight reads:

      n4   flat-only 5,081   hier-only 4,124   asymmetry 1.23x   PASS

  against the 11.26x that the same leg reported one round earlier.

  SO THE FILTER WAS NOT ONLY SUPPRESSING THE MAGNITUDE -- IT WAS
  MANUFACTURING THE ASYMMETRY, because it removed flat-side and hier-side
  candidates at different rates. Every asymmetry figure quoted before this
  point (2.2x, 5.5x, 7.33x, 11.26x, 58.6x) is an artefact of a broken
  comparison and none of them should be cited.

  THE HONEST STATE: the directional skip defect is NOT ESTABLISHED. It may
  not exist. n4's 1.23x PASS is itself a lower-bound reading -- that leg
  still carried 1,221 segment rejections -- so the correct summary is that
  NO READABLE VERDICT EXISTS YET IN EITHER DIRECTION, and the three FAIL
  verdicts that preceded it were printed over denominators of 11, 2 and 19
  by a guard that tested the sum instead of the divisor.

So the disagreement population was ~90% arithmetic and ~10% defect, and for
six rounds it was treated as 100% defect.

WHAT WENT WRONG FOR SIX ROUNDS, AND IT WAS ONE THING
====================================================

94% of disagreements sat on cell boundaries. Every hypothesis was
reverse-engineered from that number.

But a disagreement is OBSERVABLE only where the two walks straddle a plane --
floor() absorbs any difference everywhere else. So for ANY perturbation
whatever, disagreements concentrate on boundaries BY CONSTRUCTION. The 94%
was the observability window. It could not have come out otherwise, and it is
what a null result looks like.

Four mechanisms were proposed from it. THREE WERE REAL DEFECTS, correctly
found and fixed -- which is exactly what made the method feel productive.
NONE of them moved the population:

  duplicated expression    falsified by measurement
  chunk-membership alias   real, fixed, population unmoved
  corner clip              real, CONFIRMED (100% of thin clips on hit bricks
                           disagree, ten windows), and worth 0.6%
  +Y face asymmetry        an artefact of a broken verdict block

THE TELL WAS AVAILABLE THROUGHOUT AND WAS READ AS ENCOURAGEMENT: repeated
real fixes that do not move the population mean THE SELECTION IS DOING THE
WORK, NOT THE MECHANISM.

Recorded as doctrine above the individual entries in VoxelBrickTraverse.ush:
DO NOT MINE A SELECTION EFFECT FOR MECHANISM. The first question about any
concentration is not what causes it, but what else the number could have
been. If the answer is "nothing", it is not evidence.

THE SENTINEL, which cost four more legs
=======================================

VoxelMarchTraverseBrick sets H.Voxel = StartVoxel before it walks, so ON A
MISS the hit-voxel field holds the ray's ENTRY voxel with t = 0 -- a large,
plausible, perfectly legal coordinate. Three consumers forgot to gate on
bHit, and the arbiter's first four legs read 30-47% "ill-posed" as a result.

The guard that already existed was ASYMMETRIC, and that is the lesson: the
brick-mismatch assertion read `Class != 0`, excluding `lost` rays because
somebody had noticed THEY have no hier voxel. The identical hole in the other
direction -- `gained` rays having no FLAT voxel -- was never seen.

  WHEN YOU FIND YOURSELF EXCLUDING ONE DIRECTION OF A SYMMETRIC RELATION,
  THE OTHER DIRECTION IS ALREADY BROKEN.

After the fix, ill-posed collapsed to 7.7-9.0% and ties went from ZERO to
14-21% -- the grazing population the broken comparison had been discarding.

THE NEW GATE
============

v4, stated in VoxelMarchRenderer.h. Flat-as-truth is retired. The criterion
is the UNCONTESTED asymmetry, with hier-only as the control rather than zero:

  PASS when flat-only <= 1.5 * hier-only

Currently FAILS at 2.2x - 6.7x. Two guards: no verdict above 25% ill-posed
(those rays are drawn from the uncontested population too and suppress the
very counts being read), and no ratio below 20 uncontested rays.

WHAT IS NOT WORTH DOING, AND THE ONE THING THAT IS
==================================================

RETRACTED, SAME DAY. This section originally read: "~20 rays per leg in 1.35M
is 1.5e-5 of the frame: scattered single pixels of missing terrain. NOT worth
a third brick-loop rewrite." BOTH THE COORDINATOR AND I AGREED TO STOP ON A
NUMBER THAT WAS AN ARTEFACT OF A BUG WE HAD JUST FIXED -- one round after
establishing that pre-fix counts were suppressed. The correct magnitude is
~3,870 rays per leg, 0.29% of the frame, and still a lower bound.

THE DECISION IS THEREFORE REOPENED, not settled. Whether to fix it is a
judgement to make against the corrected number once ill-posed is down, not
against 20.

The reason for caution stands on its own: the second brick-loop rewrite
regressed 188x and was reverted. But caution is not the same as "negligible",
and the earlier text conflated them.

BUT THE SEVERITY SCALES WITH LEVEL. A skipped brick is 0.8 m at level 0 and
25.6 m at level 5. The same defect that is invisible here would be a hole the
size of a building in the cascade. GATE v4 MUST RUN AT EVERY LEVEL B-2b ADDS,
and the bar must be set before that cascade is built rather than after seeing
what it gives.
