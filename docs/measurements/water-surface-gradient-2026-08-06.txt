WATER-SURFACE GRADIENT AS A FLOW-DIRECTION SOURCE -- MEASURED, AND IT FAILS
2026-08-06, wet alpine block, bake_ver 14

WHY THIS WAS RUN
----------------
docs/water-flow-effects-plan-2026-08-06.md ranks the ways to make a river look
like it is flowing. The whole ranking turned on one unmeasured number, and the
plan's own instruction was "do not write a material node until step 1 has a
number".

Option A was to derive a flow direction from the gradient of the water surface
-- the trick vanilla Minecraft uses (FlowingFluid::getFlow derives a vector from
neighbouring fluid levels rather than storing one), and the mechanism the
Dynamic Waters mod is presumed to inherit. It is FREE for us: the near-field
mesher already computes that gradient for its normal
(VoxelWaterChunkComponent.cpp:184-191) and ChunkParams.w is a documented free
per-brick float. No wire bytes, no BAKE_VERSION roll, no re-download.

Option B is to ship the bake's own D8 receiver, which is correct everywhere but
costs a BAKE_VERSION roll and ~13 GB of re-download until per-section content
addressing lands.

THE PRE-REGISTERED TEST, set before running
-------------------------------------------
  On wet cells that lie on a river centreline, a gradient-derived direction
  must exist on >= 90% of cells.

A CORRECTION TO THE PLAN'S METHOD, made before measuring
--------------------------------------------------------
The plan specified a +/-1-BRICK stencil (1.6 m). That is SUB-PIXEL against an
1875 mm water plane: both taps land on the same stored control point and the
gradient reads exactly zero almost everywhere. It would have produced a
catastrophic-looking result that was an artefact of the measurement, not a fact
about the data. The stencil used is +/-1 FINE PIXEL = 3.75 m span.

VERDICT: FAIL, 37.94% against a bar of 90%
------------------------------------------
Option A is DEAD as a direction source. The failure is concentrated exactly
where it hurts most: the centreline resolves WORSE (37.9%) than interior wet
cells generally (75.7%), and the centreline is the one place a river's flow
direction matters.

The mechanism is the depth plane's 10 mm LSB against a graded surface that is
deliberately flat along much of a reach. graded_water_surface enforces that the
surface never RISES downstream; on a low-slope reach that means it does not
change at all across 3.75 m, so the stored value is identical on both taps.

WHAT SURVIVES
-------------
The MAGNITUDE is still a free and usable SPEED proxy where it resolves (p50
9.4 m/km). Nothing here argues against using it for that.

WHAT REPLACED IT, and why it is better than the thing that failed
-----------------------------------------------------------------
riverRibbonOrient (voxel-core/include/voxelcore/riverribbon.h). A reach is an
ORDERED polyline, so its tangent is a direction; the only missing piece was its
sign. That sign comes from comparing the surface height at the two ENDS of the
reach, which reads graded_water_surface's invariant directly instead of
estimating a derivative from it.

  - It is two integers, not a finite difference, so it has no quantisation
    failure mode. The endpoints of a 3 km reach differ by metres, not by
    fractions of an LSB.
  - It is exact rather than heuristic: the invariant it depends on is the one
    the whole extent stage already rests on.
  - Reaches whose ends are level are REPORTED as flat rather than assigned a
    direction. That is standing water, and it has no flow direction because it
    has no flow.
  - Costs nothing: no new data, no bake change, no BAKE_VERSION roll.

Pinned by ribbon_orients_downstream_whichever_way_it_was_traced and
ribbon_orient_reports_flat_reaches_instead_of_guessing.

WHAT THIS DOES NOT MEASURE
--------------------------
Angular agreement against the bake's own D8 receiver. That needs rec_w dumped
from a diagnostic bake (pipeline.py computes it at :4729 and deletes it at
:4784) and is not available to a tile-reading probe. So this answers "is a
direction DEFINED here", not "is it the RIGHT direction". For Option A that
distinction never mattered -- it failed the weaker test.
