D8 DIRECTION LOCK — the 45-degree parallel lines
================================================
2026-07-31. Tool: terrain-service/tools/aspect_lock.py. Site g35, tile (-5,2),
seed 20260719, alpine, ~890 m relief in the 3.84 km measured window. Statistic
computed on the ENCODED control points, i.e. exactly the bytes that ship.

The owner's description is what broke the case open, and it falsified the
explanation I had given an hour earlier: the parallel lines run DOWNHILL at
45 degrees, sometimes one diagonal and sometimes the other, evenly spaced and
sharing direction. Contours are iso-elevation by construction, so a level-set
explanation was simply wrong. 45 degrees is the signature of an 8-neighbour
lock, and the pipeline already documents one.


1. THE MECHANISM WAS ALREADY WRITTEN DOWN
------------------------------------------
flow.py:24-25, in the code's own words:

    "single steepest neighbour -- can only route along 8 compass directions,
    and at this resolution that shows up in a hillshade as dead-straight
    45-degree channels tens of pixels long"

This was mitigated for the ACCUMULATION field (MFD, mfd_p = 1.1) and
deliberately kept for channel centrelines, which was a defensible call while
D8 only picked out a few trunk lines. Two bake_ver-4 changes then promoted it
from "picks a centreline" to "defines the carve geometry over most of a
mountain":

  * incision_mode = "profile" became the default. profile_incision solves the
    graded long profile ALONG THE D8 RECEIVER TREE (incise.py:10, 255,
    325-326), so the carve inherits the 8-direction lock everywhere it acts.
  * channel_init_area_m2 fell 1e4 -> 156. The bake_ver-5 manifest for this very
    tile records channel_cells = 61,238,394 of 67.1 M, i.e. **91% of the tile
    is above the initiation threshold**. (The source comments quote 68%; the
    measured figure on this tile is higher.)


2. THE MEASUREMENT
-------------------
Two independent statistics, since either alone can mislead:

  ASPECT LOCK. Gradient direction, 360 bins, gradient-magnitude weighted, read
  as Fourier harmonics. Real landforms are anisotropic at LOW harmonics -- a
  hillside faces one way (h1) -- so a raw histogram peak proves nothing. An
  8-direction lock is specifically h8 (period 45 deg). h4 is a grid-axis bias;
  D8 excites both, because its 4 axial and 4 diagonal steps have different path
  lengths and are therefore not equivalent directions.

  ORIENTATION LOCK. 2-D power spectrum binned by direction over [0,180) in a
  7.5-60 m annulus, same harmonics. Measures the orientation of linear
  structure directly instead of inferring it from per-cell slope.

INSTRUMENT CONTROL, and it is the reason the numbers below mean anything: on a
synthetic isotropic fractal surface (radial 1/f^1.8 spectrum, random phase, no
preferred direction at any scale, 300 m relief) the aspect statistic returns
h8 = 0.0010 against a noise floor of 0.0010 -- EXACTLY the floor, 1.0x. The
tool does not manufacture 8-fold structure from a grid, a gradient operator or
a square window. The orientation statistic is dirtier (control h4 = 0.024,
10.8x floor), so the aspect harmonic is the load-bearing one.

    tile                        a_crit    aspect h8    x floor   stripe dir
    synthetic isotropic ctrl      --       0.0010        1.0x       --
    bake-g35-acrit1e4            1e4       0.0029        2.9x       17 deg
    bake-g35-v6 (bake_ver 6)     156       0.0497       50.8x      142 deg
    bake-g35-v5 (bake_ver 5)     156       0.0676       69.2x       45 deg
    bake-g35 (2026-07-29)        156       0.1098      112.3x       90 deg
    bake-v4-plains (-55,20)      156       0.1012       58.7x      120 deg

Every bake carrying the dense network reads 50-112x the noise floor. The single
bake with the sparse network reads 2.9x, which is nothing. That is a 17-38x
separation, and it is not subtle.

The orientation test agrees and adds a detail worth more than its harmonic:
the DOMINANT STRIPE DIRECTION on the sparse-network bake is 17 degrees -- an
arbitrary angle set by the terrain, which is what a real landscape looks like.
On the dense-network bakes it snaps to 45, 90, 142 and 120 degrees. Three of
those four are exact multiples of 45.


3. WHAT IS AND IS NOT ESTABLISHED
----------------------------------
ESTABLISHED: the shipped baked surface carries a strong 8-fold direction lock
that an isotropic surface does not, it is absent from the sparse-network bake,
and its strength tracks how much of the tile the D8 receiver tree carves.

NOT ESTABLISHED, and it must be said: bake-g35-acrit1e4 differs from the v5/v6
bakes in bake_ver as well as in a_crit, so the ablation is not single-variable.
The repose-strength field added at bake_ver 5 is a smooth world-anchored noise
field with no direction quantisation anywhere in it, so it is not a plausible
source of 8-fold structure -- but "not plausible" is not "measured".

THE CLEAN CONFIRMATION, one bake each:
  (a) hold bake_ver 6 fixed and bake with channel_init_area_m2 = 1e4; or
  (b) hold everything fixed and set incision_mode = "depth", which the code
      guarantees reproduces the bake_ver-2 surface exactly and bypasses the D8
      tree entirely.
Either isolates the variable. (b) is the stronger test of the mechanism, (a) is
the stronger test of the shipped configuration.

CONFIRMED 2026-07-31, same day, by three fresh bakes of tile (-5,2) with an
IDENTICAL invocation and exactly one constant varied each time. The baseline was
re-baked rather than reused, and it reproduced bake-g35-v6 to every printed digit
(h8 0.0497, h1 0.3119, relief 887.4 m) -- so the bake is deterministic and the
earlier comparison tiles were legitimately shipped-config.

    variant (one field changed)      aspect h8   x floor   orient h4  stripe dir
    BASE, shipped bake_ver 6          0.0497      50.8x     0.1807     142 deg
    channel_init_area_m2 156 -> 1e4   0.0102      10.5x     0.0414      17 deg
    incision_mode profile -> depth    0.0159      16.2x     0.0414      18 deg

Both ablations collapse the 45-degree signature: h8 falls 3.1-4.9x, the
orientation harmonic falls 4.4x, and the dominant stripe direction leaves its
grid-locked 142 deg for ~17 deg -- the SAME arbitrary terrain-set angle in both
variants independently, arrived at by two different mechanisms. That is the
result: D8 exposure causes the lock.

RESIDUAL, stated because it bounds the fix: even with the tree bypassed the
variants read 10-16x the noise floor against the isotropic control's 1.0x, so D8
tree-carving is most of the artifact but not all of it. Two remaining suspects,
both untested: `accumulate_mfd` is itself an 8-neighbour stencil, and in "depth"
mode `stream_power` still consumes the D8-derived steepest-of-8 slope magnitude.
D-infinity on the carve will not take h8 to the floor on its own.

NEITHER ABLATION IS A SHIPPABLE CONFIGURATION, and the hillshade makes this
plainer than the numbers do. The dense network at a_crit = 156 is exactly what
bake_ver 4 added to close a measured 6-10x ridge+peak deficit against real DTMs
(alpine ridge+peak 0.0042 at a_crit 1e4 against a real Teton 0.0332). Turning it
off removes the artifact by removing the dissection with it: variants A and B
read smooth and under-featured, which is the state that was rejected on realism
grounds before this artifact was ever the subject. The fix has to KEEP the dense
network and make its routing continuous -- which is the D-infinity change, and is
why the ablations are diagnostic rather than a candidate fix.

SEPARATE SUSPECTED MECHANISM, not to be confused with this one: the plains tile
shows h16 = 0.6611, an order of magnitude above anything else measured. On
gentle ground the 100 mm wire LSB is comparable to the entire rise across a
cell -- at 2% grade over 1.875 m the rise is 37 mm, well under one LSB -- so
the encoded aspect is quantised by the CODEC rather than by D8. That would be a
second, independent direction-quantisation mechanism hitting exactly the
moderate ground where band width is widest. It has its own test (re-encode at
quant = 25 mm and re-measure) and is not evidence for or against D8.


4. WHY THIS RECONCILES THE WHOLE FAILURE HISTORY
-------------------------------------------------
Twenty-plus attempts added RELIEF -- meso bands, folded strata, repose fields,
ridge composites, couloirs, amplitude ladders, and my own v19 two-pool cap. Not
one of them removed a lock to 8 directions, because amplitude and direction are
orthogonal. A direction quantisation is invisible to every amplitude metric in
the harness, which is precisely why the metrics kept improving while the
picture did not.

It also corrects two things I told the owner today:
  * the contour explanation (parallelism from level sets) -- wrong; these lines
    are flow paths and run downhill.
  * the 0.5 m/px bake proposal -- a finer grid gives FINER 45-degree stripes,
    not fewer. Resolution does not fix a direction quantisation. That option
    should not be funded on banding grounds.
