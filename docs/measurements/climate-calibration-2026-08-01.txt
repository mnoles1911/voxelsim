Wave 1d -- the coarse model crushes climate; calibrate its OUTPUT
=================================================================
2026-08-01

WHY THIS EXISTS
---------------
Wave 1a rebuilt the conditioning from real WorldClim and Wave 1b coupled
precipitation to terrain. The coarse census still showed DESERT at 0.00% and
SAVANNA at 0.00%. Precipitation was not the cause: 16.7% of land was already
arid (<400 mm). classifyBiome needs precip < 400 AND temp >= 24 C, and land
temperature never reached 24 C.

  ASKED (finalized sketch)  temp p5/p50/p95  -4.3 / 20.4 / 28.4   max 33.9
  DELIVERED (coarse model)  temp p5/p50/p95   1.5 / 13.9 / 20.5   max 24.7

Both tails pulled in; ~58% of the asked spread survives. Not altitude: at

The model hard-caps land temperature at ~21 C p95 / ~25 C max EVERYWHERE. The
conditioning asks for a tenth of the continent as desert and the model returns
none of it, in every window. NO CHOICE OF PREGEN ORIGIN FIXES THIS.

Note precipitation, at the SAME cond_snr of 0.1, is only mildly compressed
(asked arid 35-46%, delivered 15-21%). So this is not generic conditioning
weakness -- it is specific to temperature.

THE FIX, AND WHY THIS ONE
--------------------------
A fixed, global, strictly monotone remap of the model's OUTPUT, applied in
adapt_raster_to_tile before quantization.

  * MONOTONE => spatial structure is exactly preserved. The map cannot move a
    warm cell somewhere else, only relabel how warm it is. This matters
    because Wave 1b's whole point is that precipitation correlates with
    terrain; a non-monotone map would scramble that.
  * FIXED AND GLOBAL => it cannot seam. A per-tile empirical quantile match
    WOULD seam badly: each tile has its own local distribution, so the same
    physical temperature would encode differently across a tile border.
  * OUTPUT SIDE, not input side. Pre-inflating the conditioning to compensate
    would push it further out of the model's training distribution, which is
    the mechanism that was already suspected of causing the compression.
  * Raising obedience does NOT work and was measured: cond_snr[0] 0.30 -> 0.15
    REDUCED high ground (see elevation-tails-2026-08-01.txt).

Fitted by pairing pooled DELIVERED land quantiles against pooled ASKED land
quantiles over 10 windows. All four climate channels are compressed; all four
are calibrated. Every curve is strictly monotone (asserted in tests).

  channel              delivered p5/p50/p95  ->  after      target
  temperature              2.75/14.54/20.51  ->  -2.90/21.20/28.53   -2.90/21.19/28.53
  seasonality           502.7/757.9/1138.4   ->  329.9/664.6/1406.5  329.9/664.6/1406.5
  precipitation         250.8/680.7/2281.6   ->   36.2/553.9/2787.5   36.1/553.9/2787.5
  precip_variability      16.1/50.4/96.2     ->   10.5/46.4/112.3     10.5/46.4/112.3

EXTRAPOLATION IS LOAD-BEARING. np.interp CLAMPS outside its anchors, which
would pin every delivered temperature above the 99th-percentile anchor to a
single value -- a hard ceiling at 30.5 C, exactly where the desert-forming
cells live. apply_climate_calibration extends the end segments' slopes
instead, keeping the map monotone over the whole real line. There is a test
for this specifically.

CAVEATS
-------
1. The SAVANNA figures are a JOINT result. biome.h was being reworked
   concurrently -- note precip_seasonal_high_pct replacing the old
   seasonal_high -- and world_map.py parses the header live, which is why
   SAVANNA is non-zero even in the RAW column. 25.90% is high against Earth's
   ~10-20% and TEMPERATE_FOREST at 8.06% is low; treat both as provisional
   pending that work's own report.
2. These curves are fitted against THIS model, THIS conditioning stats file,
   and elev_gain=1.6. All three move them. Re-fit if any changes.
3. Measured on the COARSE stage only. The latent and decoder stages need a
   GPU and were not run; they could reintroduce compression at finer scale.
   The 2 km-relief statistic on delivered tiles remains unmeasured.
4. The calibration is hashed into provider_id -- the CURVES, not just the
   on/off flag -- so re-fitting cannot silently change every tile's climate
   under an unchanged id. Same lesson as synthetic_map_stats.json.
