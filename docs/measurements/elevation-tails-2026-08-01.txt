Wave 2b -- elevation tails: the stated diagnosis was WRONG
==========================================================
2026-08-01

THE CLAIM BEING TESTED
----------------------
docs/worldgen-variety-plan.md, Wave 2b: "the model compresses the tails, not
the sketch. The cached table implies 22.6% of land above 1000 m; delivered is
1.4%. Likely mechanism: sqrt(5814) = 76.2 normalises to +2.87 sigma, well
outside training, so the denoiser regresses to the mean."

MEASURED: THE MODEL OBEYS THE TABLE
------------------------------------
Coarse stage only, 64x64 coarse cells (492 km per side), seed 20260719,
orographic on, power 2.0. "TABLE asks" is the fraction of above-sea-level
quantile KNOTS above 1000 m -- granular at 1/64, so read it as a proxy, not a
precise figure. "MODEL delivers" is measured on coarse output.

At gain 1.0 the table asks for 22.2% of land above 1000 m and the model
delivers 24.31% -- it slightly OVER-delivers. There is no tail compression to
fix. The world already spans -6341 m to +4799 m at the coarse tier.

WHERE THE "1.4%" CAME FROM -- A CONFLATION
-------------------------------------------
The 1.4% figure is real and correctly computed. It is from the plan's own
relief measurement: "Local relief per 2 km window on land: p50 394 m, 96.6%
above 100 m, 1.4% above 1000 m."

That is RELIEF WITHIN A 2 km WINDOW. The 22.6% is ELEVATION ABOVE 1000 m.
Different quantities, different units of meaning; comparing them produced a
compression that does not exist. The plan then built Wave 2b on it.

This is the fourth time in this project a metric that was real, correctly
computed, and measuring the wrong quantity has driven a decision. See
voxelsim-banding-3d-density-blindspot for the worst case.

WHAT IS ACTUALLY WRONG IS THE 2 km RELIEF, AND THIS LEVER DOES NOT FIX IT
-------------------------------------------------------------------------
Short-range dissection is produced by the latent + decoder stages and the
bake, not by the coarse elevation marginal. Stretching the quantile table
raises PEAKS; it does not carve valleys between them. Wave 3 (coarse shaping
octaves) and Wave 4 (province-varying erosion) are the levers that touch
relief. Do not expect this one to move it, and do not re-run this measurement
expecting it to.

COND_SNR DOES NOT HELP, AND ITS DOC WAS BACKWARDS
--------------------------------------------------
world_pipeline.py:985,942:  t = atan(cond_snr);
                            cond = cos(t)*sketch + sin(t)*noise
So snr -> 0 is PURE SKETCH. LOWER IS TIGHTER. worldgen-levers.md:108 said the
opposite ("Elevation at 0.3 is loose... Raise it if hand-drawn coastlines are
being ignored") and has been corrected.

Tightening it does NOT increase relief -- the opposite. At gain 1.6, dropping
cond_snr[0] from 0.30 to 0.15 took land above 1 km from 27.05% to 24.87% and
land fraction from 40.5% to 37.7%. Tighter obedience to the sketch means less
model-added structure. Shipping keeps 0.30.

WHAT SHIPS, AND WHY
-------------------
elev_gain = 1.6, elev_gain_power = 2.0.

  v' = v + (gain-1) * vmax * (v/vmax)^power,  applied to knots above v=0 only.

Identity at v=0, so sea level and every coastline are untouched. Strictly
increasing for gain > 1, power >= 1, so the table stays monotone (np.interp
requires it). power > 1 concentrates the gain in the upper tail: mid
elevations barely move while high ground grows a lot, which is "extreme
terrain becomes more common" rather than "everything gets taller".

1.6 over 2.0: at 2.0 the table asks for 11628 m and the model returns 8144 m,
so it is visibly clipping -- we would be paying in out-of-distribution
conditioning for relief the model declines to deliver. 1.6 asks 9303 and gets
7465, a much smaller gap.

Note the coarse tier is a 7.68 km cell MEAN. In-game peaks sit above these
numbers once the fine tier and bake add relief on top, so 7465 m here is
Everest-class terrain in the world, not a 7465 m summit.

STILL UNKNOWN
-------------
Whether the stretch survives the latent + decoder stages, which were NOT run
here (they need a GPU; this box is CPU-only torch on an AMD card). The coarse
sketch only weakly steers them. The 2 km-relief statistic on delivered tiles
is the measurement that would settle it, and it needs pod time.
