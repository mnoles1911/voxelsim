So the fork took 5.6x the chunks and spread them across the levels exactly as

IT IS NOT CORRECTNESS. failed=0 across 105 and 102 five-second windows,
markTimeouts=0, gpuLatencyTimeouts=0, blindInFlight=0, bandCache=5088, and
voxel.GPU.VerifyCoarse proves L0-L4 bit-exact against coarseColumns +
makeCoarseBrick on columns, cells AND quads.

IT IS LATENCY. submit->deliver max went 90.9 ms (L0 only) -> 4,025.5 ms (L0-L4),
and the fork sits at 19-20 in flight against a cap of 256 -- waiting, not
saturated.

THE CAUSE IS THE ONE THE PLAN NAMED AND D4 NEVER BUILT: batching. A region
dispatch is a brick-aligned slab with a one-brick halo, so meshing a single 32^3
chunk dispatches 48^3 voxels -- 3.4x waste -- and D3's split readback adds a
second round trip per chunk. Against ~24 CPU workers each finishing a chunk in
~1 ms, one queue of ~28 ms round trips loses however exact it is.

CONSEQUENCE: voxel.Stream.GPUMeshMaxLevel defaults to 0. The levels are
available (-VoxelGpuMeshMaxLevel=4) for measurement, and nothing becomes 3.4x
slower merely by passing -VoxelGpuMesh. The cap for any future default is 4, not
5: L5 passes on [origin] and its [far-negative] dispatch is refused because that
fixture collapses to 2 coarse bricks of z, so L5 is UNPROVEN rather than broken.
