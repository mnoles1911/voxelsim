THE MID-GRADE "TRANSITION BAND" IS A COARSE-TIER PROBLEM, NOT A FINE-TIER ONE
=============================================================================
Measured 2026-07-30 at worldgen v15, BAKE_VERSION 4.

v14's verification recorded a residual in the 20-25% grade band -- moderate 84.0%
and third-class 63.3% of the domain stranded -- and called it the next thing to
fix. That was measured on the COARSE tier, and generalising it to the shipping
configuration was wrong. Six fine tiles were baked across the grade range to check
it, and on the fine tier the client is not the problem anywhere in that band.

Fine tier, carrier -> amplified, v15:

  site          grade   carrier                    amplified
  g10 (-7,2)    10.1%   1881 / 73.5% /  51.4 m     373 / 73.2% / 49.9 m
  g16 (-6,3)    16.4%   1163 / 80.0% /  71.0 m     342 / 81.1% / 61.5 m
  g21 (-5,3)    21.2%      1 /  0.05% / 203.6 m      1 /  0.80% / 201.6 m
  g25 (15,55)   24.6%     11 / 59.5% / 123.8 m      14 / 61.6% / 124.0 m
  g41 (-5,15)   40.6%      0 /  0.0% / 216.6 m       0 /  0.0% / 218.8 m

The amplified column tracks the carrier at every grade -- within 1.2 points
everywhere, and at g10 the client's surface has FEWER interior sinks than the
carrier it was built on (373 against 1881). Whatever stranding remains at 10-16% is
the BAKE's, and the client is faithfully reproducing it.

AND IT IS NOT KEYED ON GRADE, which is why "transition band" was the wrong name.
It tracks COASTLINE:

  site   land%   min elevation   carrier stranded
  g10     98.5%       -504 m          73.5%
  g16     69.8%      -2395 m          80.0%
  g21    100.0%        +20 m           0.05%
  g41    100.0%       +171 m           0.0%

The two tiles with sea in them strand 73-80% of the domain; the two that are
entirely land strand essentially nothing, and they sit on either side of the
supposed band (21.2% and 40.6%). A tile with coastline has genuinely closed basins
near sea level, and this metric counts them as stranded whether or not that is a
defect -- water that reaches the sea has not been "stranded" in any sense a player
would notice.

SO THE OPEN QUESTION CHANGED SHAPE. It is no longer "why does the client break the
20-25% band" -- it doesn't. It is "are the closed basins on coastal tiles real
coastal landforms or artifacts", and the drainage metric cannot answer that,
because it scores a legitimate coastal flat identically to a field of noise pits.
That is a question for a picture and a human, and it is what the shape survey
(tools/shape-survey.ps1) exists to put in front of one.

WHAT THIS DOES NOT RETRACT. The coarse-tier ladder is unchanged and still shows the
client stranding 84-92% at 6-25% grade where the carrier drains well (gentle 49,-4
is 100% land with a carrier at 17.3% and an amplified surface at 91.8%). On a
COARSE world that is a real defect and the engagement ramp is genuinely partial
there. It matters less than it looked only because the fine tier is what ships.
