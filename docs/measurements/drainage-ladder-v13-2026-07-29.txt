THE PLAINS ROW INVERTS, and it is the most useful row here. At 10 cm postings a
1.7% grade is flat: the carrier alone strands 99.0% of the domain with a mean
flow path of 4 cm. The client's detail band takes that to 97.5% and a 35 cm path
-- it IMPROVES drainage on flat ground, because giving near-level ground some
relief is what gives water anywhere to go. This is the microrelief band doing
exactly the job it was added for.

So the defect is STEEP-GROUND ONLY, and the fix must be too:

  * above roughly 25% grade the carrier is perfectly drained (0 sinks, 0%
    stranded) and the client destroys it -> cap detail here;
  * below roughly 12% grade the carrier cannot drain at 10 cm regardless and the
    detail helps -> leave it alone. A cap proportional to carrier gradient with
    no floor would remove the one thing helping here.

That is measured justification for the floor in
detailGradient <= max(gradFloor, k * carrierGradient), rather than the
hand-waving justification it had when it was proposed.

BOTH FIELDS ARE SAMPLED IDENTICALLY, checked rather than assumed, because an
unfair comparison here would invent the entire finding: sampleAmplifiedRaster
and sampleCarrierRaster (voxel-core/bench/terrainprobe.cpp:853-873) read raw
`surfaceMm` and `carrierMm` in millimetres on the same lattice with the same
stride, and NEITHER quantises to voxel heights.

---------------------------------------------------------------
Measurable only after terrainprobe's --fine-dir was fixed to bind the whole run
rather than just the calibration block (58f00e2). `config.pixel_size` reads 1875
and `band.fine_tier_table` reads 1 in every run below.

  class              carrier sinks/stranded/path      amplified sinks/stranded/path
  plains   ( 1.7%)   14095 / 89.0% /  19.1 m           9036 / 97.3% /  3.7 m
  third    (24.6%)      11 / 59.5% / 123.8 m           1620 / 94.2% / 15.9 m
  alpine   (40.6%)       0 /  0.0% / 216.6 m           1761 / 93.1% / 15.5 m

TWO THINGS HERE CHANGE THE FIX.

1. THE FINE TIER IS THE HARDER CASE, not the easier one. Alpine amplified goes
   87.9% stranded on the coarse tier to 93.1% here, with the mean flow path
   halving from 28.9 m to 15.5 m. Deleting the 25.6 m and 6.4 m octaves buys
   nothing because {3200, 900} restores comparable gradient, and the carrier now
   carries real 1.875 m structure for the detail to fight at closer wavelengths.
   Any hope that shipping the fine tier fixes sub-30 m realism on its own is dead.

2. THE FLOOR'S JUSTIFICATION IS TIER-SPECIFIC, which was not visible before.
   On the coarse tier sampled at 10 cm, a 1.7% plain's carrier is undrainable
   (99.0% stranded, 4 cm paths) and the detail band IMPROVES it to 97.5% and
   35 cm -- so a cap with no floor would remove the only thing helping. On the
   FINE tier the same plain's carrier drains: 19.1 m paths, because
   BAKE_VERSION 4 carves sub-metre swales on plains (profile_regional_p = 2.0),
   and the detail band then DEGRADES it to 97.3% and 3.7 m.

   So the detail helps flat ground only where the carrier has nothing at that
   scale. Where the bake supplies sub-metre structure, the client's job is to
   stay out of its way. That is the plan's "complement, do not add" principle
   arriving as a measurement instead of an intention, and it means the cap should
   bite at every class on a fine world while sparing flat ground on a coarse one.
