THE MESO BAND: GRADE VARIATION AT THE BANDING WAVELENGTH, BAKE-SIDE, DRAINAGE-CLEAN
===================================================================================
2026-07-31. Follow-up to repose-field-2026-07-30.txt, after the owner's live
verdict on bake_ver 5: the repose field is confirmed where thermal binds
(gullies), the banding on open steep faces is not fixed, and partial
improvement is not acceptance.

THE TARGET, restated from the diagnosis both prior docs converge on: band
spacing = 100 mm / local grade, so banding is legible wherever grade is
near-constant over tens of metres, and NOTHING in the pipeline varied grade at
the 10-60 m wavelength on translation-invariant expanses -- thermal is
translation-invariant there (shed == receive), incision needs drainage to act,
and every client-side attempt died against the drainage cap.

WHAT SHIPS (all bake-side, bake_ver 6; the client is BIT-EXACT worldgen v18):

  1. STRENGTH-MODULATED INCISION (noise.repose_erodibility ->
     incise.profile_incision(erodibility=...)): the same material-strength
     field that keys thermal's threshold now multiplies erodibility,
     log-linear, pinned at 1.0 on baseline rock. Strong strata hold their bed
     (knickpoints), weak bands cut treads -- slope varies at strata wavelength
     wherever there is any drainage at all, not only where thermal moves mass.
     RATIO 3, NOT 6, and the reason is the codec: at 6 the treads it makes
     are flat enough to fall under the wire format's 100 mm quantization
     floor, and the RECONSTRUCTED carrier grows pits the raw surface does not
     have (measured: 1 carrier sink at ratio 6, 0 at ratio 3, same window).
  2. B4 MESO RELIEF (noise.meso_relief): 15 m / 11.25 m octaves, RMS 0.8 /
     0.4 m, gated to steep ground (slope 0.20-0.40 ramp), world-anchored,
     applied POST-THERMAL -- the one slot where the relief survives (thermal
     never sees it) -- then drainage-reconciled (below) and refilled before
     encode. This is the band that varies grade at the banding wavelength on
     exactly the faces that band.
  3. B4b MICRO-REFILL + DESCENT ENFORCEMENT (pipeline.bake_padded_domain):
     after the meso field is added, every cell is raised until it keeps at
     least min(its pre-meso drop, 0.11 m) over its pre-meso D8 receiver --
     zero pits structurally -- then the B2a epsilon fill re-runs as belt and
     braces.

THE FALSIFIED LEVERS, because three of the four ranked candidates died by
measurement and the reasons are worth more than the code:

  * CLIENT MESO OCTAVES (12.8/6.4 m in the fine shaping band, under a
    two-pool v14 cap that gave them their own gradient budget): they DO vary
    grade -- and strand 0.04-0.33% of the fine repro site, realization-
    dependent (1-6 sinks, up to 390 mm deep). A band coherent over 6-13 m
    makes pits the drainage lattice RESOLVES; every earlier sub-metre band
    was "safe" only because its pits were smaller than the lattice. A
    point-sampled gradient cap cannot prevent a 13 m feature from damming the
    dip beside it. Withdrawn.
  * THE SECOND (LANDFORM-SCALE) WARP: 24.6 m lattice, throw swept 1.0-3.25 m
    -- 0.3-3.2% stranded at EVERY setting (v16's shipped 4 m component reads
    0.0%). Mechanism: the warp compresses the carrier gradient by up to its
    own peak derivative, the cap prices detail against the UNCOMPRESSED
    slope, and the capped ladder's true peak runs ~1.9x nominal; where the
    two coincide, downhill reverses in basins metres wide. Withdrawn; the
    negative result is recorded in carrier.h's carrierWarpPx block.
  * THE TWO-POOL CAP ON THE COARSE TIER: with no client meso band left to
    fund, its only measured effect was moving the coarse ten-class ladder
    against the documented v14 posture (steepest 17.5 -> 22.9% stranded,
    moderate 83.4 -> 89.5). Withdrawn with the rest of the client change.

THE LESSON THAT COST THE MOST ITERATIONS: **THE CODEC IS A STAGE.** Every
pre-existing pass was implicitly codec-safe -- incision creates real
gradients, thermal drives toward a threshold, the fill's epsilon flats get
carved through by incision afterwards. B4 is the first pass whose output goes
to the encoder with no process behind it, and THREE distinct sub-LSB failure
classes appeared before the guarantee held:
  1. a 7.5 m octave (4 cells/wavelength -- exactly the B-spline floor)
     produced a 230 mm basin BETWEEN the native samples via prefilter
     ringing; no fill on the sample lattice can repair what the sample
     lattice cannot see (it stranded 10.4% of the window through a sub-pixel
     sill in a trunk channel). Fixed by moving the octave to 11.25 m
     (6 cells), verified by a half-pixel pit census on the DECODED tile.
  2. meso-flattened channel reaches under 100 mm/cell reconstruct as +-50 mm
     noise and dam even when no float-precision pit exists. Fixed by gating
     on min(local, D8-downstream) slope -- a bed must read its own profile,
     not its walls -- with the gate slope field ERODED (9-cell min) and
     SMOOTHED (5-cell) so full-amplitude band content never sits one cell
     from a gentle reach, plus the descent enforcement.
  3. using the 0.11 m codec-proof step as the REFILL's flood epsilon domed
     every wide flat it refloods (lake floors tilted into 5-17 m cones,
     flat-shaded scars in the hillshade). The refill keeps the auto epsilon;
     the 0.11 m bound lives only in the enforcement, which takes
     min(original drop, 0.11) and therefore preserves gentle reaches exactly.
