Ring gap -- root-caused and fixed. Baseline, two regressions-and-fixes, and the
resurrection/fork-cap sweep that shipped, all on REAL terrain.

Anchor: -VoxelSpawnAt=-84480,53760 (tile-coverage centre -- see
docs/ring-gap-handoff.md for why origin spawns were flat-plane and worthless).
Flight: -VoxelPerfFlight=line (new) -- 90 s preflight pinned at spawn while the
cascade warms, 120 s flight at 20 m/s heading +X, 60 s linger pinned. Edit log
cleared before every COLD-FILL leg (ground rule 11); flight legs ran with no
edits in-session, so there was nothing to clear.

"residue" = the converged `Voxel coverage holes=` count read during the linger
phase, i.e. after the cascade has had 60 s stationary to catch up -- this is the
PERMANENT hole count, not the transient in-flight one. "hitches" / "chunks" /
p50 / p95 are the VoxelPerfRun complete line. GPU = mesh fork default-on; CPU =
-VoxelNoGpuMesh. voxel.Stream.CoverageVerify=1 (new) drives the holes count.

Verdict evidence:

  * Flight-phase eviction bursts, new evictions/level line: R1 ~650 per 5 s
    window, R2 ~300, R3 ~140.
  * capRel (the 5 s retention-cap release) dominates flight-phase retention
    releases in every other leg, BUT the C1 leg (LodRetentionMs=20000) had
    capRel=0 in flight while holes were UNCHANGED. The cap is not the visible
    driver.
  * covRelAbsent (the absent-record release) = 6-19k per flight.
  * recordsAdded 2.3-3.4M against 110-125k dispatched per flight: two orders of
    magnitude of admission churn for every chunk actually sent to a mesher.
  * Residue is DETERMINISTIC: byte-identical hole coordinates across 4
    independent runs, at r=65 m and r=124-128 m (both R1).

Also on record from these legs:

  * CPU beat GPU under motion in every axis measured (hitches, p95, residue).
  * Hitch attribution: GPU hitch frames are render-thread-BUSY dominated
    (median renderMs 75 ms vs 3 ms CPU; RHI flat 1.7 ms both configs). That is
    fork BOOKKEEPING -- 2 RDG graphs/frame, an O(256) poll walk, ~18 release
    commands/frame -- not GPU execution time.

Flight-PHASE transient holes (the honest ancestor-walk verifier, sampled

CPU median 55 (was 921 pre-resurrection -- a >16x cut), GPU median 854-907

kills the PERMANENT ring, not the seconds-of-latency gap at the leading edge
while a chunk is queued and in flight to a mesher. It converges to 0 by the
time the linger phase reads it. Closing THAT gap is a throughput problem --
see the cold-fill sections below for where the throughput levers are.
