WHAT FRAGMENTS THE BAKED RIVER
seed 20260719, tiles (-11,-4) (-11,-5) (-12,-5) (-11,-6), bake_ver 10, 2026-08-04
================================================================================

THE CAUSE: THE TWO NETWORKS WERE CUT AT DIFFERENT HEIGHTS
----------------------------------------------------------
The recorded gap -- area network 4 components over 2 km with a largest span of
4,216 m against a wet mask of ~2,000 components, longest 1,113 m, and only 36.1%
of above-threshold area cells wet -- is an artifact of the COMPARISON, not a
defect in the bake. `flow log2 >= 23` and `q >= q_drawable` are not the same
threshold. Level-matched they swap, and most of the gap closes.

  q_drawable                              3.1467e6 m3/yr
  corridor median implied runoff q/acc    0.1883 m/yr
  => q_drawable expressed as an AREA      1.671e7 m2 = 2^23.99
  the control was cut at                  2^23      = 8.389e6 m2
                                          ---------------------
                                          1.99x apart

Stitched corridor, one grid, 8-connected, the same labeller the record used.
Component spans computed for EVERY component of >= 40 px, not a top-5 shortlist:

                                    cells   comps  largest    span    >=1.8km  >=2km
  AREA as reported   acc>=2^23     76,439   3,632   8,823 px  4,216 m     5       4
  AREA re-cut at the wet level     29,159   1,298   1,792 px  2,188 m     1       1
  Q as reported      q>=q_drawable 29,159   2,019   1,719 px  1,113 m     0       0
  WET shipped plane                27,347   2,014   1,719 px  1,113 m     0       0
  Q  re-cut at the 2^23 level      87,102   4,808   8,644 px  4,216 m     3       1

  wet share of the area network as reported  35.1%   (the record's 36.1%)
  wet share of the LEVEL-MATCHED area network 66.4%

("Re-cut at the wet level" is the area threshold admitting the SAME NUMBER of
cells as the wet cut: 1.6916e7 m2 = 2^24.01, within 1% of the 2^23.99 the median
runoff implies. Two independent ways of matching the level, same answer.)

APPORTIONING THE 4,216 m -> 1,113 m COLLAPSE, in log span:

  threshold LEVEL      4,216 -> 2,188 m    49%   3 of the 4 >=2 km components
  runoff WEIGHTING     2,188 -> 1,113 m    51%   the last one
  B5 basins                    0 m          0%
  the log2 bin                 0 m          0%

The runoff-weighting half is NOT the boundary term task #49 replaced. It is
`discharge_source`'s per-cell `runoff_m * cell_m^2` seed: q/acc is the
CATCHMENT-MEAN runoff, and its median differs 2.8x across the four tiles
(0.0971, 0.1790, 0.1975, 0.2705 m/yr), so one Q threshold is a spatially
varying AREA threshold -- 2^23.47 on (-11,-5), 2^24.95 on (-11,-4). Where it
rides near the local channel size it fragments; where it sits well above, it
does not.

AND THE RECORD'S OWN JUSTIFICATION FOR CHOOSING 23 IS OFF BY AN OCTAVE. The
flow log2 of the corridor's wet cells:

  log2  22      23      24      25     26     27
  wet   552   7,302  10,237  6,946  1,706    391
         2.0%  26.7%   37.4%  25.4%   6.2%   1.4%

The median wet cell sits at log2 24. "log2 >= 23, where wet cells begin" is a
whole octave below where they actually begin -- which is exactly the factor of
two above.

CANDIDATE 1 -- THE log2 BIN. RULED OUT, AND PROVABLY SO
--------------------------------------------------------
`floor(log2 x) >= 23` is `x >= 2^23` IDENTICALLY: a threshold placed on a bin
EDGE is exact, and the bin's width can only blur cells inside the set, never at
its boundary. So the byte cannot smooth the network at the threshold, whatever
it does elsewhere. Measured rather than left as an argument, per tile, the
byte-binned network against the unquantised field:

  tile        flow byte log2>=23        acc >= 2^23              identical?
  (-12,-5)   11,960 / 301 / 4,216 m   11,960 / 301 / 4,216 m     to the cell
  (-11,-4)   22,883 / 366 / 2,207 m   22,883 / 366 / 2,207 m     to the cell
  (-11,-5)   14,718 / 1,000 / 692 m   14,718 / 1,000 / 692 m     to the cell
  (-11,-6)   24,014 / 1,948 / 987 m   26,878 / 1,965 / 987 m     2,864 cells

The one difference is fully accounted for: all 2,864 cells are BELOW SEA LEVEL
(elevation -476.0 to -199.5 m, p50 -288.1 m), where the encoder writes flow = 0.
Zero cells above sea level differ, and the longest span is 987 m in both arms.

Contribution to the gap: none.

Basin exclusion removes 6% of the cells and 0 m of span.

CANDIDATE 2 -- MFD SPLITTING Q. REAL, LARGE, AND NOT THE AREA/WET GAP
----------------------------------------------------------------------
It is the mechanism by which ANY threshold on this field breaks a reach. It is
not what separates the area network from the wet mask, because it acts on both
identically: q and acc are two sweeps of the same kernel over the same surface,
and they fall together at the same cells with the same ratios, equal to three
decimals on every tile (e.g. (-12,-5): acc falls at 25.45% of downstream steps
with p5 ratio 0.702; q falls at 25.45% with p5 ratio 0.702).

PROOF ON THE SHIPPED FIELD, NOTHING RECONSTRUCTED. Water leaves a cell
downhill, so under any SINGLE-receiver rule some strictly-lower neighbour must
hold at least what the cell holds -- the receiver inherits all of it. Cells on
the area network with a lower neighbour, none of which holds as much:

  (-12,-5)   3,040 of 11,947   25.45%
  (-11,-4)   6,620 of 22,873   28.94%
  (-11,-5)   4,872 of 14,709   33.12%
  (-11,-6)   8,232 of 26,870   30.64%

A quarter to a third of the network splits its entire accumulation. Under D8
this count is identically 0.

THE CONTROL. Same refilled surface, same cell-area seed, same kernel family,
only the routing rule differs, both cut to the wet mask's own cell count:

  tile          n        MFD p=1.1                 D8 (monotone)
  (-12,-5)   6,747   196 comps / 3,667 m       1 comp  / 7,616 m
  (-11,-4)   6,001   111 comps /   915 m       1 comp  / 10,473 m
  (-11,-5)   9,623   619 comps / 1,808 m       3 comps / 8,003 m
  (-11,-6)   6,788   572 comps /   514 m       3 comps / 5,898 m

100x to 600x the component count, and 2x to 11x the span.

THE CAVEAT, STATED RATHER THAN BURIED. This control refills the SHIPPED,
post-B5, interior-only surface; the bake accumulated over the padded, pre-B5
one with the pyramid's inflow at its rim. The reconstruction's accumulation is
therefore not the bake's -- log-ratio against it is p50 +0.35 to +1.66 and p95
+12.6 to +14.0. Both arms of the A/B run over the IDENTICAL reconstruction, so
the comparison is internally valid and is about the routing rule alone, but its
absolute spans are not the bake's and are not quoted as such. The
reconstruction-free measurement above is what carries the claim on the shipped
field.
