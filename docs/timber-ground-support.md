# Bounded ground support for falling environment assets

The collision bridge now sizes its footprint from timber geometry instead of assuming every tree fits on a 32 m square. A hinged trunk reserves its full diagonal rotation radius around the stump. A released body reserves its half-diagonal radius around its center of mass. Both include four metres of margin and the union with a two-second linear velocity sweep. Prepared restore geometry, hinge position and saved velocity determine the footprint before publication.

Ground is constructed as 3.2 m sections at 20 cm sampling. A shared per-world frame gate permits at most 64 terrain samples and one 512-triangle collision cook per frame across restoration and live timber. Sampling has a soft 1 ms limit; an individual terrain query or collision cook is not interruptible. Each section checks fine-terrain residency before sampling, including its boundary. Missing or nonresident terrain defers construction without requesting synchronous terrain generation. Completed footprints are reused only when they contain the entire requested rectangle.

The timber pre-physics tick checks support before advancing simulation. If coverage is incomplete, it disables simulation and preserves linear/angular velocity, awake state and hinge. It recreates the constraint binding and restores velocities once coverage is ready. Persistence captures the saved velocities while paused. Sleeping timber retains this tick so later wakeups cannot silently leave coverage. Bridge restoration waits for the prepared body's footprint before enabling physics. Legacy PrepareGround now schedules the same bounded work instead of cooking an entire square synchronously.

There are at most 32 cached footprints and 16,384 reserved sections total per world. A roughly 400 m square is the single-footprint upper bound; actual admission includes rounding and shares the budget with other bodies. One unused proxy may be reclaimed per call; player vicinity, live/pending timber coverage and nearby fellable assets protect proxies. If capacity or residency cannot satisfy coverage, the body stays frozen rather than falling through missing collision.

This removes the fixed-size correctness assumption, not all physics limitations. Cold large footprints can take tens of seconds or minutes to construct at the conservative frame budget. Overlapping footprints still duplicate sections; this implementation retains existing proxy ownership rather than introducing a shared section eviction system. The heightfield approximation cannot represent caves, overhangs or vertical surfaces, while edited ground depends on the world edit hook described below. Extremely fast external impulses can exceed the lookahead between ticks. These limitations need production terrain collision integration; they are not claims of general-purpose terrain physics.

`Voxel.Objects.TimberGroundBounds` covers translated 60 m geometry, conservative free-body bounds, missing resident terrain, frozen waiting and invalid/oversized requests. UE build, runtime fall verification and resident-terrain cook timings must be run by the coordinating agent; they were not executed by the implementation agent.


## Terrain edit invalidation

`NotifyTerrainEdited(World, Bounds)` marks only overlapping cached collision sections dirty, with a 20 cm halo for shared boundary samples. It resets affected in-flight samples and immediately pauses intersecting live timber. Rebuilding replaces the existing section rather than adding another component, using the same residency and global frame limits. Pending restores remain unpublished until the invalidated footprint is ready. Unbuilt sections naturally sample the new terrain. The world subsystem calls this hook from level-zero `MarkChunkDirtyForRemesh`, covering grouped local and replicated edits. Startup replay precedes ground creation; future mutation paths must preserve this invalidation. No new resident-world cook/rebuild automation was run for this checkpoint.
