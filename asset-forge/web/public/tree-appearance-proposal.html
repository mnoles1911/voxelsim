Existing game cutout experiment

Historical September 6 oak demonstration, not the newly reviewed tree collection. This shows the existing renderer experiment we can reuse and improve. The snow background is the old test scene; the proposal remains spring.

What the pilot must prove

Matched Forge/game appearance; recognizable species in a mixed stand; no noisy bark; readable limbs through leaves; stable shadows and distant crowns; persistent seeds and decisions; measured GPU cost. Ordinary terrain trees still need production rendering integration.

Decision: approve or decline a 24-model pilot. After appearance approval, apply the update across existing variants. Preserve all endorsements and rejections. Production rendering must pass its integration checks.
