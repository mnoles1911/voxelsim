# Tree art baseline

All tree assets are modeled in spring. This applies to textures, coloring,
foliage and new growth across the tree library. Use species-appropriate spring
appearance rather than a uniform green tint or universal flowering treatment.

Spring photographs are the reference for finished foliage and color. Photos
from other seasons may support branch, trunk and crown-structure review only.
Seasonal transitions and seasonal recoloring are out of scope.

The shared baseline is recorded in `forge/forest_profiles.py` and carried into
species reference-review records and newly installed species records. Recording
the baseline does not certify that existing pilots meet it; visual review remains
required before replacing an existing collection.
